# Day 2 — Script

**Duration:** ~30 seconds  
**Format:** Face to camera, same raw energy  
**Theme:** What happened after Day 1 — the reaction, the feeling, the truth

---

## Option A: The Aftermath (Recommended)

> Kal pehla video daala tha. Jaante ho uske baad kya hua?
>
> Har 2 minute mein phone check kar raha tha. Kitne views aaye? Kisi ne kuch bola? Kisi ne mazaak udaaya?
>
> Aur phir realize hua — yahi toh problem hai. Main woh kar raha tha jo sab karte hain — dusron ki validation pe jeena.
>
> *"What others think of you is none of your business."*
>
> Aaj ka fear — logo ki opinion aur task usko ignore karna. Mushkil hai. But try kar raha hoon.
>
> Day 2.

---

## Option B: The Mirror

> Kal video post karke ek cheez samajh aayi —
>
> Camera mein apni shakal dekhna aur apni awaaz sunna — yeh sabse uncomfortable cheez hai.
>
> Hum sab apne aap se bhaag te hain. Mirror mein dekhte hain toh baal theek karte hain. But khud ko SUNNA? Apne thoughts sunna? 
>
> Woh darr alag level ka hai.
>
> Day 2. Aaj khud se bhaagna band.

---

## Option C: The Honest Check-In

> Day 2. Honest update.
>
> Kal ka video daala. Kuch logo ne dekha. Kuch logo ne ignore kiya. Duniya nahi badli.
>
> Aur wahi toh point hai — hum sochte hain sab dekh rahe hain. Reality? Kisi ko itna farak nahi padta.
>
> Yeh sunne mein bura lagta hai. But iska matlab yeh bhi hai — tum jo chahte ho woh kar sakte ho. Koi itna dhyan nahi de raha jitna tum sochte ho.
>
> Day 2. Darr thoda kam. Thoda.

---

## My Pick: Option A

It continues the story naturally. Day 1 you posted, Day 2 you tell what happened. The audience is now invested in the series — "what happened next?" Plus the validation-seeking confession is painfully relatable for your age group.
