# Day 3 — Script

**Duration:** ~30-35 seconds  
**Format:** Face to camera OR film yourself doing the thing, then react after  
**Theme:** First real-world action — moving from talking about fear to DOING something scary

---

## Option A: The Stranger Challenge (Recommended)

> Day 3. Aaj kuch alag kiya.
>
> Aaj ek anjaan insaan se baat ki. Matlab bilkul stranger. No reason. Bas jaake bola — "Bhai, ek minute. Tumhara din kaisa ja raha hai?"
>
> Jaante ho kya hua? Usne mujhe 5 minute apni life ke baare mein bataya. Ek stranger. Bas kyunki maine poocha.
>
> Hum sab apne phone mein ghuse rehte hain aur sochte hain — "log judge karenge." Reality? Log baat karna CHAHTE hain. Koi poochta nahi isliye nahi karte.
>
> *"Every person you meet is fighting a battle you know nothing about. Be kind."*
>
> Day 3. Fear faced — anjaan se baat karna. ✅

---

## Option B: The Voice Note

> Aaj ek experiment kiya.
>
> Apne best friend ko ek voice note bheja — "Bhai, main tujhe seriously batata hoon, tu meri life mein important hai."
>
> Simple. Seedha. Bina mazaak ke.
>
> Uska reply? ... *[pause]* ... 10 second silence, phir usne bhi kuch aisa bheja jo usne kabhi nahi bola.
>
> Hum itne uncomfortable kyun hain genuine hone mein? Kyun hum har baat ko joke mein convert karte hain?
>
> Day 3. Fear — bina mask ke baat karna.

---

## Option C: The Speaking Aloud

> Aaj maine ek kaam kiya — akele khade hokar 2 minute bola. Awaaz se. Room mein.
>
> Koi sun nahi raha tha. Koi nahi tha. Phir bhi haath kaanp rahe the.
>
> Imagine karo — akele room mein, koi nahi hai, phir bhi darr lag raha hai.
>
> Yeh darr camera ka nahi hai. Yeh darr apni khud ki awaaz sunne ka hai. Apne thoughts ko real banaane ka hai.
>
> Jab tak tum bolo nahi — tumhari soch sirf tumhare andar hai. Bolte hi — woh real ho jaati hai. Aur woh darawna hai.
>
> Day 3.

---

## My Pick: Option A

You've talked about fear for 2 days. Day 3 needs ACTION that the audience can see. "Maine aaj stranger se baat ki" — that's proof. That's content that makes people think "wow, yeh toh actually kar raha hai." It separates you from every other "motivation" account.

**Pro tip:** If possible, film the BEFORE (your nervous face before approaching) and AFTER (your reaction after the conversation). That before/after is gold content.
