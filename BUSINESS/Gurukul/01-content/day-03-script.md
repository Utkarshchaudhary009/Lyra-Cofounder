**Duration:** ~35-40 seconds  
**Format:** Face to camera, chill energy — like talking to a friend  
**Theme:** What's coming next — bringing the audience INTO the journey with you

## Script

> Day 3. Aur aaj kuch alag —
>
> Do din ho gaye hain aur ab main aur tum — discuss karte hain ki next 18 din mein kya hone waala hai. Kyunki yeh sirf "roz video daalna" nahi hai.
>
> Number 1 — mera ek extempore competition aa raha hai. Stage. Mic. Log. Aur main woh insaan hoon jisko camera ke saamne bolne mein darr lagta hai. Toh ham saath mil kar uski preparation karenge — main share karunga kaise speeches likhta hoon, meri journey, aur kyun main yeh sab kar raha hoon.
>
> Number 2 — main ek book padh raha hoon — Zero to One by Peter Thiel. Usme jo cheezein mujhe acchi lagi — woh share karunga.
>
> Number 3 — is journey ke baare mein baat karunga. Kya kar raha hoon isko better banane ke liye, aur kya obstacles aa rahe hain raaste mein.
>
> Basically — risk hai toh ishq hai. 😎
>
> Day 3 coming to an end. Bye.
