# Day 5 — The Confidence Scam

**Duration:** ~40 seconds  
**Format:** Face to camera, raw, confused, real energy  
**Theme:** What confidence actually means (hint: it's not the absence of fear)

---

## Script

> **[Close-up, dead honest face]**
>
> "Day 5 and I still feel like an idiot every time I hit record."
>
> **[Pull back, casual sitting]**
>
> "People keep saying — *be confident, be confident.* But like… what does that even mean?"
>
> "Because if confidence means not being scared — then I'm definitely not confident. I'm still nervous every time I hit record."
>
> **[Small awkward laugh]**
>
> "But then… I'm still here? Day 5. I showed up again."
>
> "So either I'm confident and I don't know it… or confidence isn't what I thought it was."
>
> **[Pause, genuinely thinking]**
>
> "I don't know. Maybe confidence isn't some switch that turns on one day. Maybe it's just… doing the thing even when you feel stupid doing it."
>
> "I feel stupid right now, by the way."
>
> **[Small smile]**
>
> "Day 5."

---

## Caption
Confidence isn't the absence of fear. It's doing it anyway.

Day 5/21. Still terrified. Still showing up.

## Shooting Notes
| Element | Tip |
|---|---|
| **First frame** | Close up, looking slightly awkward/honest. |
| **Pacing** | Don't rush it. Let the pauses land. It should feel like you're figuring it out as you speak. |
| **Tone** | Not preachy. Just thinking out loud. |

---

## Flow
```
Hook (feel like an idiot) → What is confidence? → I'm not confident (still scared) → But I'm still here → Redefining confidence (doing it even when feeling stupid) → Day 5
```
