# Day 7 — Why Most Speeches Are Boring in 5 Seconds

**Duration:** ~40 seconds  
**Format:** Face to camera, teaching from personal experience  
**Theme:** How to open a speech — from quotes to storytelling

---

## Script

> **[Hook: Close-up, matter-of-fact tone]**
>
> "I figured out why most speeches are boring in the first 5 seconds."
>
> **[Pull back, casual]**
>
> "When I started speaking — every single speech I gave, I opened with a quote. *'As Abraham Lincoln once said…'* *'As APJ Abdul Kalam once said…'*"
>
> "It felt safe. It sounded smart. And honestly? It worked… until I realized every other person on stage was doing the exact same thing."
>
> **[Slight head shake]**
>
> "Five speakers. Five quotes. Nobody remembers any of them."
>
> "Then I discovered something way more powerful — storytelling."
>
> "Instead of starting with someone else's words… start with YOUR moment. Something that happened to you. Something real."
>
> "Don't say *'Failure is the stepping stone to success.'* Say *'In 8th grade, I walked on stage with my paper and got last place.'*"
>
> **[Snap/point at camera]**
>
> "See the difference? One sounds like a textbook. The other one — you actually want to hear what happens next."
>
> "A quote tells people what to think. A story makes them feel it."
>
> **[Direct eye contact]**
>
> "Day 7."

---

## Caption

Stop opening your speech with someone else's words. Open with YOUR story.
