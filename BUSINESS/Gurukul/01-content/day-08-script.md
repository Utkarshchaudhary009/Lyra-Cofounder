# Day 8 — Want the Right Thing

**Duration:** ~40 seconds  
**Format:** Face to camera, insight through analogy  
**Theme:** Two things matter — getting what you want, and wanting the right thing

---

## Script

> **[Hook: Close-up, calm confident tone]**
>
> "Every cricketer wants to be the next Virat Kohli. But most of them are chasing the wrong thing."
>
> **[Pull back, storytelling energy]**
>
> "Think about it. A kid starts playing cricket. He wants to hit sixes. He wants the crowd to cheer. He wants to play for India."
>
> "But the kids who actually make it? They didn't care about the crowd. They cared about their technique. Their footwork. Their discipline at 5 AM."
>
> "They wanted the RIGHT thing — the process. Not just the result."
>
> **[Shift to personal, grounded tone]**
>
> "When I started this challenge, I set only two goals for myself — get comfortable with the camera, and improve my speaking. Not followers. Not fame."
>
> "And honestly? That's the only reason I'm still going."
>
> **[Direct eye contact, hold]**
>
> "In life, only two things matter. Getting what you want — and making sure you want the right thing."
>
> "Day 8."

---

## Caption
Every kid wants to be Virat Kohli. But the ones who make it aren't chasing the crowd — they're chasing the process.

8 days in. No followers goal. No viral goal. Just two things — camera comfort and speaking skills.

That's it. And that's enough.

## Shooting Notes
| Element | Tip |
|---|---|
| **First frame** | Serious face + text: *"MOST CRICKETERS ARE CHASING THE WRONG THING"* |
| **Cricket section** | Slightly animated, paint the picture — sixes, crowd, India |
| **Personal shift** | Slow down. Drop the energy. Make it real and quiet. |
