# Day 9 — "The One" Rule for Speeches

**Duration:** ~40 seconds  
**Format:** Face to camera, confident teaching energy  
**Theme:** How to structure a speech around a single core idea

---

## Script

> **[Hook: Holding up two fists/hands]**
>
> "A bad speech talks about 10 things. A good speech talks about one thing — 10 different ways."
>
> **[Drop hands, casual]**
>
> "I've been writing speeches for a while now, and I realized every great speech has what I call 'The One.'"
>
> "The One is the central idea your entire speech wraps around. A question. A quote. A story. Pick one."
>
> "You open with it. You build around it. And you come back to it at the end. That's the whole structure."
>
> "Martin Luther King — *'I have a dream.'* That's it. That's his entire speech in 5 words. Every paragraph, every story, every emotion — it all came back to that one line."
>
> **[Direct eye contact]**
>
> "Find your 'One' — and your speech writes itself."
>
> "Day 9."

---

## Caption
A bad speech gives you 10 points to remember. A great speech gives you 1 idea you can't forget.

Before you write your next speech, find "The One". 🎤

## Shooting Notes
| Element | Tip |
|---|---|
| **First frame** | Hands up, confident posture + text: *"THE SECRET TO GREAT SPEECHES"* |
| **Pacing** | Say "A bad speech talks about 10 things" fast. Say "A good speech talks about one thing" slow and deliberate. |
| **The MLK Example** | Bring your energy up here to show passion for the example. |

---

## Flow
```
Hook (10 things vs 1 thing) → Introduce "The One" → What is it? (quote, story, question) → The structure (open, build, close) → Example (MLK) → Lesson (speech writes itself) → Day 9
```
