# 💡 Content Ideas Bank

Whenever you're stuck, come here. Pick one. Record it. Don't overthink.

---

## 🎤 Raw Talking (Just You + Camera)
- [ ] Something school never taught me
- [ ] The dumbest mistake I made this week
- [ ] Why I started this challenge
- [ ] A conversation that changed how I think
- [ ] What I'd tell my 14-year-old self
- [ ] The thing I'm most afraid of right now
- [ ] Why most people my age are stuck
- [ ] An opinion I have that nobody agrees with
- [ ] What "confidence" actually means (not what people think)
- [ ] The difference between smart and wise

## 📖 What I Read / Watched / Heard
- [ ] One idea from [book] in 60 seconds
- [ ] "I saw this reel and here's why it's wrong"
- [ ] A quote that hit different — and WHY
- [ ] Something my parent/teacher said that I finally understand
- [ ] A podcast clip that broke my brain + my take

## 🔥 Real Life Moments
- [ ] I tried talking to a stranger today — here's what happened
- [ ] I got rejected/embarrassed — here's the truth
- [ ] A fight I had and what I learned from it
- [ ] I asked someone a question nobody asks
- [ ] Something awkward happened and I filmed the aftermath
- [ ] "I called someone I haven't spoken to in years"

- [ ] "I'm 17 and I built this" — fast build video

- [ ] This bug took 4 hours — here's the lesson

- [ ] Coding at 2AM — the raw reality

- [ ] "Everyone says X but nobody talks about Y"
- [ ] How I actually make decisions
- [ ] Debating both sides of an argument with myself
- [ ] "The lie we all believe about [topic]"
- [ ] What I think success actually looks like

## 🔁 The Universal Template (When Nothing Else Works)

> "I used to think [X]. Then [something happened]. Now I think [Y]."

30-60 seconds. Works every time.
