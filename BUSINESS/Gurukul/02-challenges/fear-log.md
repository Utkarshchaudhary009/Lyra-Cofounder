# 😨 Fear Log

> Every fear you confront becomes content. Every fear you avoid stays a wall.

Track every fear here. Before and after. This becomes your personal proof of growth — and the raw material for your best posts.

---

## How To Use
1. Write the fear BEFORE you do it
2. Rate your terror (1-10)
3. Do it
4. Write what actually happened
5. Rate how you feel AFTER (1-10)
