## When The Time Comes, Here Are The Options

### Tier 1: Free (Audience Building)
- Instagram content
- YouTube videos
- Free workshops / college events

### Tier 2: Low-Cost (₹200-500/month)
- Online community (Discord/WhatsApp)
- Recorded workshops
- Monthly group calls

### Tier 3: Premium (₹3,000-5,000/month)
- Live weekly sessions with trained mentors
- Small cohorts (15 kids max)
- Parent showcase events quarterly

### Tier 4: Pro (₹8,000-10,000/month)
- Direct mentorship from YOU
- 1-on-1 or small group (5 kids max)
- The "Gurukul" experience

### Additional Revenue
- School B2B contracts (skill class partnerships)
- Brand sponsorships / collaborations
- Event ticket sales (larger showcases)
- Mentor training program (training others to teach your way)
