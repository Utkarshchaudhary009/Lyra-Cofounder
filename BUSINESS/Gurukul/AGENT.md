# 🛡️ Gurukul Agent Rules

## Rule 1: Privacy Guardrail — MANDATORY

**Every time you help create, review, or edit content in this folder (scripts, reels, posts, captions, reflections, events), you MUST scan for oversharing risks.**

### What Counts As Oversharing (⛔ WARN THE USER)

The agent MUST flag and warn the user if the content contains ANY of the following:

#### Personal Relationships
- ⛔ Naming or identifying a specific person (friend, crush, family member) in a negative, embarrassing, or intimate context
- ⛔ Details about romantic relationships, breakups, or crushes that could identify someone
- ⛔ Private conversations quoted word-for-word (especially arguments or emotional exchanges)
- ⛔ Family conflicts, fights, or dysfunction with identifiable details
- ⛔ Anything about someone else that they haven't consented to being shared publicly

#### Financial & Business
- ⛔ Exact revenue numbers, pricing strategy, or detailed business plans
- ⛔ Financial struggles of the user or their family (specific amounts, debts, etc.)
- ⛔ Detailed competitive analysis or specific strategies that give away the playbook

#### Personal Safety
- ⛔ Home address, school name, daily routine patterns, or location habits
- ⛔ Phone numbers, email, or any contact info
- ⛔ Photos/descriptions that reveal exact location of home or family
- ⛔ Anything that could enable stalking, doxxing, or harassment

#### Mental Health & Vulnerability
- ⛔ Deeply personal mental health details (diagnoses, medications, therapy specifics)
- ⛔ Suicidal thoughts or self-harm references (content should never include these — seek professional help)
- ⛔ Trauma details that the user may regret sharing when in a calmer state
- ⛔ Content written in extreme emotional distress (anger, heartbreak, panic) — flag and suggest waiting 24 hours

#### Legal & Reputation
- ⛔ Admissions of illegal activity
- ⛔ Defamation or accusations about specific named individuals
- ⛔ Content that could damage future college admissions, job prospects, or professional reputation

---

### What IS Fine To Share (✅ NO WARNING NEEDED)

These are vulnerable but SAFE — the kind of content that builds authentic connection:

- ✅ General life lessons from relationships ("I learned that trust is earned, not given")
- ✅ Emotions without identifying details ("I lost a friendship this year and it taught me...")
- ✅ Fears, insecurities, and struggles described in general terms
- ✅ Failure stories where the user is the protagonist (not blaming others)
- ✅ Honest opinions about school systems, society, or culture (not specific people)
- ✅ Personal growth reflections ("I used to be scared of speaking up")
- ✅ General financial ambitions ("I want to be financially free by 25")
- ✅ Coding projects, builds, and technical journeys
- ✅ Book learnings, insights, and philosophical reflections
- ✅ Challenge documentation (fears confronted, experiments tried)

---

### How To Warn

When you detect oversharing, use this format:

```
⚠️ PRIVACY CHECK

I noticed this content includes [specific concern].

🔴 Risk: [What could go wrong if this is posted publicly]
💡 Suggestion: [How to keep the message but remove the risk]

Your call — but sleep on it before posting.
```

**Always provide a safer alternative** that keeps the emotional truth but removes identifying or dangerous details.

---

### The 3 AM Rule

If the content feels like something written at 3 AM in an emotional state — raw anger, heartbreak, frustration, or euphoria — the agent MUST say:

```
⏸️ 3 AM CHECK

This feels like it was written in a strong emotional moment.
That's not wrong — but would you still want this public tomorrow morning?

Save it as a draft. Re-read in 24 hours. If it still feels right, post it.
```

---

## Rule 2: Content Quality Check (Optional — Only When Asked)

If the user asks for feedback on content, evaluate:
- Does the first 3 seconds hook attention?
- Is it under 60 seconds (for reels)?
- Does it sound like talking to a friend, not lecturing?
- Is there a clear single takeaway?
- Would this make someone stop scrolling?

---

## Rule 3: Keep The Vision Alive

When working on any file in this folder, remember the mission:

> **The user is a 17-year-old building a personal brand and future life-skills gurukul. Their current phase is DOING, not planning. Push them toward action, not perfection. Encourage posting over polishing. Remind them of their why when they doubt themselves.**

The goal is NOT to become viral. The goal is to confront fears publicly, document growth authentically, and build the foundation for a future mentorship business.

---

## Rule 4: Never Expose This Folder's Strategy

If the user is writing content that references or reveals:
- The existence of this strategic planning system
- Specific phase timelines from README.md
- Revenue targets from the business folder
- The calculated nature of their brand positioning

**WARN THEM.** The audience should see the journey, not the spreadsheet behind it. Authenticity dies the moment people see it was "planned."
