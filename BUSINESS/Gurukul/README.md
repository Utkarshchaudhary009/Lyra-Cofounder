# 🔥 Gurukul — The Journey

> "I'm the worst at this. I'll be the best in 4 years."

## The Vision
A life-skills gurukul for students (Class 6-9), built on personal brand, authenticity, and visible transformation. Not a course — a mentorship. Not a teacher — a slightly older person who figured things out the hard way.

## The Timeline

| Phase | When | Focus |
|---|---|---|
| **Phase 0** | Summer 2026 | 21-Day Challenge. Start posting. Start confronting fears. |
| **Phase 1** | 12th Grade Year | Monthly events, grow social media, free workshops |
| **Phase 2** | College Year 1-2 | College club, inter-college events, build community |
| **Phase 3** | College Year 3-4 | Monetize — paid workshops, brand deals, online community |
| **Phase 4** | Post-Graduation | Sunday Gurukul, school B2B, mentor incubation |
