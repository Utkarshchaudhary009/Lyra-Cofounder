# Chemical Kinetics — The Complete Mastery Book

## Class 12 Physical Chemistry (NCERT Ch. 4) | Board + JEE Mains

***

## Who Is This Book For?

You're a Class 12 student. You've memorised the formula for a first-order half-life. You know what a rate constant is. And yet — when the examiner gives you experimental data and asks for the order, or asks what happens to the rate when volume is halved, you get stuck.

**This book fixes that.**

It doesn't hand you formulas to blindly plug into calculators. It builds your **chemical intuition** — so you can look at a table of concentrations and rates, instantly spot the patterns, and solve any question the Board or JEE throws at you without breaking a sweat.

***

## How This Book Works

### The 8-Stage Method (Per Chapter)

Every chapter follows the same ruthless progression:

| Stage                               | What You Do                                                     | Why It Works                                                   |
| ----------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **🎯 Stage 1: The Core Idea**       | Read an analogy-driven, story-based explanation                 | You understand *why* the concept exists, not just what it is   |
| **🔬 Stage 2: The Formula Lab**     | See every formula derived, with units and common trap callouts  | You'll never mix up units or forget a minus sign               |
| **🧱 Stage 3: Type-wise Mastery**   | 4–8 problem types, each with a solved example + 4–5 practice Qs | You build *atomic skills* — one clean pattern at a time        |
| **🔀 Stage 4: Type Mixer**          | 3–5 problems combining 2–3 types from the same chapter          | You learn to identify *which type* a question belongs to       |
| **📋 Stage 5: Board Arsenal**       | NCERT + Board-style questions with model answers                | Clean, direct — score these first every time                   |
| **🚀 Stage 6: JEE Mains Arena**     | 5 MCQs with traps + full explained solutions                    | The real test of understanding                                 |
| **🧠 Stage 7: Assertion-Reasoning** | Statement & A-R questions with detailed explanation             | Trains you to dissect tricky phrasing and detect subtle errors |
| **🏆 Stage 8: MCQ Mastery**         | 15 curated MCQs targeting every trap in the chapter             | The final boss — combine speed, accuracy, and concept clarity  |

### Difficulty Tags

Every problem is tagged:

- 🟢 **Easy** — Direct formula, solve in under 1 minute
- 🟡 **Medium** — 2–3 steps, \~2–3 minutes
- 🔴 **Hard** — Multi-concept, traps, 3–5 minutes
- ⭐ **Must-Do** — This exact pattern repeats in real exams

### The Rules

1. **Never skip Stage 3.** Even "obvious" types need hand practice.
2. **Struggle before checking solutions.** At least 5 minutes of honest effort.
3. **Mark wrong questions.** Revisit after 3 days.
4. **Follow the sequence.** Don't jump to Integrated Rate Equations without mastering Rate Law first.

## Prerequisites

Before starting, make sure you are comfortable with:

1. **Stoichiometry** — Balancing equations and relating moles
2. **Basic Logarithms** — $\log(ab)$, $\log(a/b)$, $\log(a^n)$, $\ln x = 2.303 \log x$
3. **Basic Calculus** — Understanding $dx/dt$ as a rate of change (slope)
4. **Graphs** — Equation of a straight line ($y = mx + c$)

## Notation Used in This Book

| Symbol             | Meaning                                                           |
| ------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------- |
| $r$ or Rate        | Rate of reaction                                                  |
| $k$                | Rate constant (velocity constant or specific reaction rate)       |
| $\[A]$             | Molar concentration of reactant A ($\text{mol L}^{-1}$)           |
| $\[A]\_0$ or $a$   | Initial concentration of reactant                                 |
| $\[A]\_t$ or $a-x$ | Concentration of reactant at time $t$                             |
| $x$                | Amount of reactant reacted at time $t$                            |
| $n$                | Order of reaction                                                 |
| $t\_{1/2}$         | Half-life period                                                  |
| $E\_a$             | Activation energy                                                 |
| $A$                | Arrhenius factor (Frequency factor / Pre-exponential factor)      |
| $R$                | Universal gas constant ($8.314 \text{ J K}^{-1} \text{mol}^{-1}$) |
| $T$                | Temperature in Kelvin                                             |
| $\Delta H$         | Enthalpy of reaction                                              |
