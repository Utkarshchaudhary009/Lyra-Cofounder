## Section A: Multiple Choice Questions (20 × 4 = 80 marks)

**Q1.** A solution is prepared by dissolving 4 g of NaOH in 500 mL of water. The molarity of the solution is:
(1) 0.1 M   (2) 0.2 M   (3) 0.4 M   (4) 0.5 M

**Q2.** The vapour pressure of a solution containing 2 moles of a non-volatile solute in 8 moles of solvent is 80 mm Hg. The vapour pressure of the pure solvent is:
(1) 100 mm   (2) 80 mm   (3) 120 mm   (4) 90 mm

**Q3.** Which has the maximum osmotic pressure at the same temperature?<br>
(1) 0.1 M glucose   (2) 0.1 M sucrose   (3) 0.1 M NaCl   (4) 0.1 M BaCl₂

**Q4.** 100 mL of 0.1 M H₂SO₄ is diluted to 1 L. The molarity of the diluted solution is:
(1) 0.01 M   (2) 0.001 M   (3) 1.0 M   (4) 0.1 M

**Q5.** The molality of 0.5 M H₂SO₄ (density = 1.03 g/mL) is approximately:
(1) 0.49 m   (2) 0.50 m   (3) 0.52 m   (4) 0.48 m

**Q6.** The relative lowering of vapour pressure of a 0.1 M aqueous solution of a non-electrolyte is approximately:
(1) 0.1   (2) 0.0018   (3) 0.01   (4) 0.18

**Q7.** If K_f = 1.86, the freezing point of 0.1 m glucose solution is:
(1) −0.186°C   (2) −1.86°C   (3) −0.093°C   (4) 0°C

**Q8.** For an electrolyte AB₃ that dissociates into A³⁺ and 3B⁻, the van't Hoff factor when α = 0.75 is:
(1) 2.5   (2) 3.25   (3) 4.0   (4) 1.75

**Q9.** An azeotropic mixture of two liquids boils at a temperature lower than either of them when it shows:
(1) Negative deviation   (2) Positive deviation   (3) Ideal behaviour   (4) No deviation

**Q10.** The mole fraction of ethanol in a solution containing 46 g ethanol and 90 g water is:
(1) 0.167   (2) 0.25   (3) 0.5   (4) 0.33

**Q11.** 0.1 m MgCl₂ freezes at −0.465°C. K_f = 1.86. The van't Hoff factor i is:
(1) 2.0   (2) 2.5   (3) 3.0   (4) 1.5

**Q12.** Which of the following mixtures will NOT form an azeotrope?<br>
(1) Ethanol + water   (2) Acetone + chloroform
(3) Benzene + toluene  (4) HNO₃ + water

**Q13.** The Henry's law constant for O₂ in water is 4.34 × 10⁴ atm at 25°C. The mole fraction of O₂ in water when the gas is at 0.5 atm (assuming air is 20% O₂) is:
(1) 2.3 × 10⁻⁶   (2) 4.6 × 10⁻⁶   (3) 1.15 × 10⁻⁶   (4) 9.2 × 10⁻⁶

**Q14.** A solution of 0.5 g of a substance in 50 g of water gives ΔT_b = 0.26°C. If K_b = 0.52, the molar mass of the substance is:
(1) 10 g/mol   (2) 20 g/mol   (3) 50 g/mol   (4) 100 g/mol

**Q15.** Assertion <br>
(A): On adding NaCl to water, the boiling point increases.
Reason (R): The vapour pressure of the solution decreases due to addition of non-volatile solute.
(1) Both true, R explains A   (2) Both true, R doesn't explain A
(3) A true, R false   (4) A false, R true

**Q16.** Which of the following aqueous solutions will have the lowest vapour pressure at 25°C?<br>
(1) 0.1 M glucose   (2) 0.1 M NaCl   (3) 0.1 M BaCl₂   (4) 0.1 M AlCl₃

**Q17.** The degree of association of benzoic acid (dimer) in benzene is 80%. The van't Hoff factor is:
(1) 0.6   (2) 0.5   (3) 0.8   (4) 0.2

**Q18.** A 0.01 M solution of KCl shows an osmotic pressure of 0.4926 atm at 300 K. The van't Hoff factor is:
(1) 1.5   (2) 2.0   (3) 1.0   (4) 2.5

**Q19.** The vapour pressure of pure A is 100 mm and pure B is 200 mm. A mixture of A and B has a total vapour pressure of 150 mm. The mole fraction of B in the liquid phase is:
(1) 0.25   (2) 0.50   (3) 0.75   (4) 0.33

**Q20.** The molar mass of a solute determined by osmotic pressure measurement is 60,000 g/mol. The same solute by freezing point depression gives a molar mass of 120,000 g/mol. This indicates that the solute:
(1) Dissociates   (2) Associates into dimers   (3) Is a non-electrolyte   (4) Is impure
