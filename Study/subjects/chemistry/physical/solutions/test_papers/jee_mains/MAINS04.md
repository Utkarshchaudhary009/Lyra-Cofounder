## Section A: Multiple Choice Questions (20 × 4 = 80 marks)

**Q1.** The molarity of pure water at 4°C is:
(1) 55.5 M   (2) 18 M   (3) 1 M   (4) 100 M

(1) 0.02   (2) 0.98   (3) 0.04   (4) 0.01

(1) Equal to P°_solvent
(2) Less than P°_solvent
(3) Greater than P°_solvent

**Q4.** Which of the following aqueous solutions will have the minimum freezing point?<br>
(1) 0.01 m NaCl   (2) 0.01 m CaCl₂   (3) 0.01 m AlCl₃   (4) 0.01 m glucose

**Q5.** The van't Hoff factor for a completely dissociated K₃[Fe(CN)₆] is:
(1) 3   (2) 4   (3) 5   (4) 6

**Q7.** Three solutions of glucose have concentrations: 0.1 M, 0.2 M, and 0.3 M. The ratio of their osmotic pressures at the same temperature is:
(1) 1:2:3   (2) 3:2:1   (3) 1:1:1   (4) 1:4:9

**Q8.** Which of the following statements about azeotropes is correct?<br>
(1) They boil at a definite temperature
(2) They have variable composition in vapour phase
(3) They cannot be separated by fractional distillation

**Q9.** The cryoscopic constant K_f for water can be expressed in:
(1) K kg mol⁻¹   (2) mol kg K⁻¹   (3) K mol kg⁻¹   (4) K⁻¹

**Q10.** The density of a 0.5 M solution of a solute (MM = 100) is 1.05 g/mL. Its molality is:
(1) 0.5 m   (2) 0.48 m   (3) 0.52 m   (4) 0.55 m

**Q11.** A 0.1 m solution of a non-electrolyte in water freezes at −0.186°C. The K_f of water is:
(1) 1.86   (2) 0.186   (3) 18.6   (4) 0.0186

**Q12.** The degree of dissociation of a weak electrolyte can be calculated from van't Hoff factor using:
(1) α = (i−1)/(n−1)   (2) α = (n−1)/(i−1)   (3) α = 1 + (n−1)i   (4) α = i/(n−1)

**Q13.** At 25°C, the vapour pressure of a solution of 1 mole of non-volatile solute in 9 moles of water is 22.0 mm. The vapour pressure of pure water at this temperature is approximately:
(1) 24.4 mm   (2) 22.0 mm   (3) 20.0 mm   (4) 25.0 mm

**Q14.** Assertion <br>

**Q15.** Which of the following colligative properties is most suitable for determining the molar mass of a protein?<br>
(1) RLVP   (2) ΔT_b   (3) ΔT_f   (4) Osmotic pressure

**Q16.** A 0.1 M solution of a weak base BOH has a degree of dissociation of 4%. Its van't Hoff factor i is:
(1) 1.04   (2) 1.4   (3) 0.96   (4) 1.0

**Q18.** A solution containing 10 g of a non-electrolyte in 100 g of water has a boiling point of 100.26°C. K_b = 0.52. The molar mass of the solute is:
