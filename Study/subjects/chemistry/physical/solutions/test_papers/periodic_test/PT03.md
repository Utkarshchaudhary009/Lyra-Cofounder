**Q10.** What are ideal solutions?<br> State the conditions for a solution to be ideal. A solution of liquids A and B with P°_A = 200 mm Hg and P°_B = 100 mm Hg has a mole fraction of A in vapour phase equal to 0.5. Find the mole fraction of A in the liquid phase.

<summary>📝 Answer Key</summary>

**Q1:** An azeotrope is a mixture of liquids that boils at a constant temperature and has the same composition in the liquid and vapour phases.

**Q2:** Osmotic pressure is the excess pressure that must be applied to a solution to prevent the inward flow of solvent across a semipermeable membrane.

**Q3:** Molality (it depends on mass, not volume; volume changes with temperature).

**Q4:** For a solution of volatile liquids, the partial vapour pressure of each component is directly proportional to its mole fraction in the liquid phase: p_A = χ_A × P°_A.

**Q5:**
- P_s = χ_B × P°_B (Raoult's law for non-volatile solute)
- χ_B = 0.8/(0.8 + 0.2) = 0.8
- P_s = 0.8 × 100 = **80 mm Hg**

**Q6:**
- Ideal solution: ΔH_mix = 0, ΔV_mix = 0, follows Raoult's law at all concentrations. (e.g., benzene + toluene)
- Non-ideal solution: ΔH_mix ≠ 0, ΔV_mix ≠ 0, deviates from Raoult's law. (e.g., ethanol + water)

**Q7:**
- π = CRT → R = π/(CT) = 2.46/(0.1 × 300) = 2.46/30 = **0.082 L atm mol⁻¹ K⁻¹**

**Q8:**
- 15.8 M means 15.8 moles in 1 L
- Mass of HNO₃ = 15.8 × 63 = 995.4 g
- Mass of solution = 1000 × 1.41 = 1410 g
- w/w% = (995.4/1410) × 100 = **70.6%**
- Mass of solvent = 1410 − 995.4 = 414.6 g = 0.4146 kg
- m = 15.8/0.4146 = **38.1 m**

**Q9:**
- π = (W/MM) × RT/V
- MM = (W × R × T)/(π × V)
- MM = (2 × 0.0821 × 300)/(0.0328 × 0.25) = 49.26/0.0082 = **6007 g/mol**

**Q10:**
Ideal solution conditions: (i) ΔH_mix = 0, (ii) ΔV_mix = 0, (iii) Follows Raoult's law over entire composition range, (iv) Similar intermolecular forces.

Given: y_A = 0.5, P°_A = 200, P°_B = 100

From Dalton's law: y_A = p_A/P_total = (χ_A × 200)/(χ_A × 200 + χ_B × 100)

Since y_A = 0.5: χ_A × 200 = χ_B × 100 → 2χ_A = χ_B

And χ_A + χ_B = 1 → χ_A + 2χ_A = 1 → 3χ_A = 1

**χ_A = 1/3 ≈ 0.333**
