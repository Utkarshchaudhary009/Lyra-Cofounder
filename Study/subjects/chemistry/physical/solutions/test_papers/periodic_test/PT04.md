### Section D: Long Answer (4 marks × 1 = 4 marks)

**Q10.** Explain the van't Hoff factor for dissociation and association. 0.5 g of KCl (MM = 74.5) dissolved in 100 g of water gave ΔT_f = 0.24°C. Calculate i and α for KCl. (K_f = 1.86)

<details>
<summary>📝 Answer Key</summary>

**Q1:** van't Hoff factor i = (Observed colligative property)/(Expected colligative property) = (Expected molar mass)/(Observed molar mass).

**Q2:** i = 2 (NaCl → Na⁺ + Cl⁻)

**Q3:** Acetone + chloroform mixture, or HNO₃ + water (solutions with strong inter-component H-bonding).

**Q4:** Osmotic pressure (since it gives measurable values even at very low concentrations).

**Q5:**
- Expected ΔT_f = K_f × m = 1.86 × 0.1 = 0.186°C
- i = Observed/Expected = 0.34/0.186 = **1.83**

**Q6:**
- For dimer (x = 2): β = 2(1 − i) = 2(1 − 0.55) = 2 × 0.45 = **0.90 = 90%**

**Q7:**
- Minimum boiling azeotrope: BP is lower than either component; shows positive deviation. (e.g., ethanol + water, 95.6% ethanol, BP = 78.1°C)
- Maximum boiling azeotrope: BP is higher than either component; shows negative deviation. (e.g., HNO₃ + water, 68% HNO₃, BP = 120.5°C)

**Q8:**
- n_urea = 5/60 = 0.0833 mol; n_water = 100/18 = 5.556 mol
- χ_urea = 0.0833/(0.0833 + 5.556) = 0.0833/5.639 = 0.01478
- RLVP = χ_urea = 0.01478
- (P° − P_s)/P° = 0.01478 → P_s = P°(1 − 0.01478) = 17.54 × 0.9852 = **17.28 mm Hg**

**Q9:**
- K₂SO₄ → 2K⁺ + SO₄²⁻, n = 3
- (a) α = (i − 1)/(n − 1) = (2.5 − 1)/(3 − 1) = 1.5/2 = **0.75 = 75%**
- (b) If α = 1: i = 3, ΔT_f = 3 × 1.86 × 0.2 = **1.116°C**

**Q10:**
- Dissociation: i > 1 (more particles, e.g., NaCl gives i ≈ 2)
- Association: i < 1 (fewer particles, e.g., acetic acid dimer gives i ≈ 0.5)
- Formula: i = 1 + (n − 1)α for dissociation; i = 1 − β(1 − 1/x) for association

Given KCl:
- Expected ΔT_f (no dissociation) = K_f × m
- m = (0.5 × 1000)/(74.5 × 100) = 500/7450 = 0.0671 m
- Expected ΔT_f = 1.86 × 0.0671 = 0.1248°C
- i = 0.24/0.1248 = **1.923**
- KCl → n = 2: α = (1.923 − 1)/(2 − 1) = **0.923 = 92.3%**
