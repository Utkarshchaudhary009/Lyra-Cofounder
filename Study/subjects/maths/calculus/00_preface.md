# Continuity & Differentiation — The Complete Mastery Book
## Class 11 → 12 (NCERT Ch. 13, 5) | Board + JEE Mains

---

## Who Is This Book For?<br>

You know what sin 30° is. You can solve quadratic equations. Maybe you've heard someone say "the derivative of x² is 2x" and nodded along. But when someone asks — *why* is the derivative of x² equal to 2x? What does it actually *mean*? Why does the function |x| misbehave at x = 0? — your confidence evaporates.

**This book fixes that.**

It doesn't dump formulas on you. It builds your **calculus intuition** — so you understand what a limit *is* before you evaluate one, what continuity *means* before you test for it, and what a derivative *does* before you compute one. By the end, you'll handle everything from NCERT boards to JEE Mains with surgical precision.

## How This Book Works

### The 8-Stage Method (Per Chapter)

Every chapter follows the same ruthless progression:

| Stage | What You Do | Why It Works |
|-------|-------------|--------------|
| **Stage 1: The Core Idea** | Read an analogy-driven, story-based explanation | You understand *why* the concept exists, not just what it is |
| **Stage 2: The Formula Lab** | See every formula derived, with edge cases and common trap warnings | You'll never confuse "limit exists" with "function is defined" again |
| **Stage 3: Type-wise Mastery** | 6–10 problem types, each with a solved example + 4–5 practice Qs | You build *atomic skills* — one clean pattern at a time |
| **Stage 4: Type Mixer** | 3–5 problems combining 2–3 types from the same chapter | You learn to identify *which type* a question belongs to |
| **Stage 5: Board Arsenal** | NCERT + Board-style questions with model answers | Clean, direct — score these first every time |
| **Stage 6: JEE Mains Arena** | 5–8 MCQs with traps + full explained solutions | The real test of understanding |
| **Stage 7: Assertion-Reasoning** | Statement & A-R questions with detailed explanation | Trains you to dissect tricky phrasing and detect subtle errors |
| **Stage 8: MCQ Mastery** | 15–20 MCQs targeting every trap in the chapter | The final boss — combine speed, accuracy, and concept clarity |

### Difficulty Tags

Every problem is tagged:

- 🟢 **Easy** — Direct formula, solve in under 1 minute
- 🟡 **Medium** — 2–3 steps, ~2–3 minutes
- 🔴 **Hard** — Multi-concept, traps, 3–5 minutes
- ⭐ **Must-Do** — This exact pattern repeats in real exams

### The Rules

1. **Never skip Stage 3.** Even "obvious" types need hand practice.
2. **Struggle before checking solutions.** At least 5 minutes of honest effort.
3. **Mark wrong questions.** Revisit after 3 days.
4. **Complete Part I before starting Part II.** Limits are the language of calculus — you can't skip the grammar.
5. **Part III chapters can be done in any order once Parts I–II are complete.**

## Prerequisites

Before starting, make sure you are comfortable with:

1. **Algebra** — Factorization, quadratic equations, completing the square, binomial expansion
2. **Functions** — Domain, range, composition, types (polynomial, rational, trigonometric, exponential, logarithmic)
3. **Trigonometry** — All 6 ratios, identities (sin²θ + cos²θ = 1), compound angle formulas, graphs
4. **Coordinate Geometry** — Slope of a line, equation of tangent/normal
5. **Inequalities** — Solving linear and quadratic inequalities
6. **Logarithms & Exponentials** — Laws of logarithms, eˣ properties

## Notation Used in This Book

| Symbol | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| lim(x→a) f(x) | Limit of f(x) as x approaches a |
| lim(x→a⁻) f(x) | Left-hand limit (x approaches a from the left) |
| lim(x→a⁺) f(x) | Right-hand limit (x approaches a from the right) |
| f'(x) or dy/dx | First derivative of y = f(x) |
| f''(x) or d²y/dx² | Second derivative |
| f⁽ⁿ⁾(x) or dⁿy/dxⁿ | nth derivative |
| Δx, h | Small change in x (used in first principles) |
| → | "approaches" or "tends to" |
| ∞ | Infinity |
| DNE | Does Not Exist |
| LHL, RHL | Left-Hand Limit, Right-Hand Limit |
| LHD, RHD | Left-Hand Derivative, Right-Hand Derivative |
| [x] | Greatest integer function (floor function) |
| {x} | Fractional part function = x − [x] |
| |x| | Absolute value / modulus function |
| e | Euler's number ≈ 2.71828... |
| ln x | Natural logarithm (base e) |
| log x | Logarithm base 10 (unless stated otherwise) |
