# Trigonometry — The Complete Mastery Book
## Class 10 → 12 (NCERT Ch. 3, 8, 9) | Board + JEE Mains

---

## Who Is This Book For?<br>

You're a student who's memorised sin²θ + cos²θ = 1. You can rattle off sin 30°, cos 45°, and tan 60° in your sleep. Maybe you've even heard of "compound angles." And yet — when the JEE paper asks you to find the general solution of sin 2x = cos 3x, or simplify tan⁻¹(1/2) + tan⁻¹(1/3), your hand freezes.

**This book fixes that.**

It doesn't hand you formulas to memorise. It builds your **trigonometric intuition** — so you can derive every identity on the spot, spot every trap in an equation, and solve any question from Class 10 boards to JEE Mains.

---

## How This Book Works

### The 8-Stage Method (Per Chapter)

Every chapter follows the same ruthless progression:

| Stage | What You Do | Why It Works |
|-------|-------------|--------------|
| **Stage 1: The Core Idea** | Read an analogy-driven, story-based explanation | You understand *why* the concept exists, not just what it is |
| **Stage 2: The Formula Lab** | See every formula derived, with unit circle callouts and common trap warnings | You'll never confuse sin⁻¹x with (sin x)⁻¹ again |
| **Stage 3: Type-wise Mastery** | 8–12 problem types, each with a solved example + 4–5 practice Qs | You build *atomic skills* — one clean pattern at a time |
| **Stage 4: Type Mixer** | 3–5 problems combining 2–3 types from the same chapter | You learn to identify *which type* a question belongs to |
| **Stage 5: Board Arsenal** | NCERT + Board-style questions with model answers | Clean, direct — score these first every time |
| **Stage 6: JEE Mains Arena** | 5–8 MCQs with traps + full explained solutions | The real test of understanding |
| **Stage 7: Assertion-Reasoning** | Statement & A-R questions with detailed explanation | Trains you to dissect tricky phrasing and detect subtle errors |
| **Stage 8: MCQ Mastery** | 20 MCQs targeting every trap in the chapter | The final boss — combine speed, accuracy, and concept clarity |

### Difficulty Tags

Every problem is tagged:

- 🟢 **Easy** — Direct formula, solve in under 1 minute
- 🟡 **Medium** — 2–3 steps, ~2–3 minutes
- 🔴 **Hard** — Multi-concept, traps, 3–5 minutes
- ⭐ **Must-Do** — This exact pattern repeats in real exams

### The Rules

1. **Never skip Stage 3.** Even "obvious" types need hand practice.
2. **Struggle before checking solutions.** At least 5 minutes of honest effort.
3. **Mark wrong questions.** Revisit after 3 days.
4. **Do Part III (Inverse Trigonometry) only after finishing Part II.**
5. **Part IV chapters can be done in any order once Parts I–III are complete.**

## Prerequisites

Before starting, make sure you are comfortable with:

1. **Pythagoras Theorem** — a² + b² = c² in right triangles
2. **Basic Algebra** — solving linear & quadratic equations, factorisation
3. **Number Line** — positive/negative numbers, inequalities
4. **Cartesian Coordinate System** — quadrants, plotting points
5. **Basic Geometry** — triangle types, angles, parallel lines

## Notation Used in This Book

| Symbol | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| sin θ, cos θ, tan θ | Primary trigonometric ratios |
| cosec θ, sec θ, cot θ | Reciprocal trigonometric ratios |
| sin⁻¹x, cos⁻¹x, etc. | Inverse trigonometric functions (NOT (sin x)⁻¹) |
| arcsin x, arccos x | Alternative notation for inverse (used interchangeably) |
| π | pi = 180° (in radians) |
| n ∈ ℤ | n is any integer (for general solutions) |
| Δ | Area of triangle |
| R | Circumradius of triangle |
| r | Inradius of triangle |
| s | Semi-perimeter of triangle |
| θ (theta) | General angle variable |
| α, β, γ | Specific angle variables |
