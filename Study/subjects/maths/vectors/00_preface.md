## 📖 About This Book

Vectors are not just a topic — they are the **language of physics and the geometry of 3D space**. Every force, velocity, acceleration, electric field, and magnetic field is expressed as a vector. Without vectors, modern science cannot speak.

This book covers **everything** — from the very first definition of a scalar and vector, all the way to JEE Advanced–level scalar triple products, vector equations of planes, and multi-concept problem strategies. It unifies:

- 📘 **NCERT Class 11 Physics** — Chapter 4 (Motion in a Plane)
- 📗 **NCERT Class 12 Maths** — Chapter 10 (Vector Algebra) + Chapter 11 (Three Dimensional Geometry)
- 🏆 **JEE Mains & JEE Advanced** — Full syllabus, beyond NCERT

Each chapter builds strictly on the previous one. Do **not** skip ahead.

## ⚠️ Top 15 Student Traps

> These are the most common mistakes that cost students marks every year. Read them **before** Chapter 1, and re-read them **after** Chapter 19.

1. 🚨 **Electric current is NOT a vector** — it doesn't obey the vector addition (parallelogram) law; it's a scalar.
2. 🚨 `|A⃗ + B⃗| = |A⃗ − B⃗|` implies **A⃗ ⊥ B⃗**, NOT that |A⃗| = |B⃗|.
3. 🚨 `A⃗ × B⃗ = A⃗ × C⃗` does **NOT** imply B⃗ = C⃗ — the difference (B⃗ − C⃗) could be parallel to A⃗.
4. 🚨 `A⃗ · B⃗ = A⃗ · C⃗` does **NOT** imply B⃗ = C⃗ — the difference (B⃗ − C⃗) could be perpendicular to A⃗.
5. 🚨 **Cross product is NOT associative** — `a⃗ × (b⃗ × c⃗) ≠ (a⃗ × b⃗) × c⃗` in general.
6. 🚨 **Scalar projection ≠ Vector projection** — one is a pure number (can be negative), the other is a vector along a direction.
7. 🚨 **A line has TWO valid sets of direction cosines**: `(l, m, n)` and `(−l, −m, −n)` — both are correct.
8. 🚨 **Zero dot product means perpendicular OR at least one vector is zero** — perpendicularity alone is not guaranteed.
9. 🚨 **Dot product result = scalar; Cross product result = vector** — mixing these up in equations is an instant error.
10. 🚨 **Scalar Triple Product (STP) can be NEGATIVE** — the sign indicates the handedness of the orientation, not an error.
11. 🚨 **In Lami's Theorem, α, β, γ are angles OPPOSITE to their respective forces**, not the angles between the forces.
12. 🚨 **Area of triangle = ½|A⃗ × B⃗|**, Area of parallelogram = |A⃗ × B⃗| — off by a factor of 2 is a classic trap.
13. 🚨 **In cross product cyclic rule: ĵ × î = −k̂**, not +k̂ — reversing the cyclic order flips the sign.
14. 🚨 **Shortest distance formula applies only to SKEW lines** — for intersecting lines, the shortest distance is zero.
15. 🚨 **Angle between planes is found using their NORMAL vectors** — not the surface direction vectors of the planes.
