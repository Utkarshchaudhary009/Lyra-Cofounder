## About This Book

This book dismantles **NCERT Chapter 2 — Electrostatic Potential and Capacitance** into **14 narrative-driven chapters**, each built around a single concept. Unlike a conventional textbook, every chapter opens with a paradox, a story, or a thought experiment — and the physics is *derived* from that narrative.

You don't memorize formulas here. You **discover** them.

---

## How This Book Works

### The Narrative Method (Per Chapter)

Each chapter follows a four-part structure designed to make physics feel like a detective novel while secretly training you for Board exams and JEE Mains.

| Phase | What Happens | Why It Works |
|-------|-------------|--------------|
| **The Paradox / The Story** | A physical paradox, historical anecdote, or thought experiment opens the chapter | You *feel* why the concept matters before touching a formula |
| **Building the Concept** | The theory is developed logically from the opening story; formulas are derived, not dropped from the sky | You understand where every equation comes from |
| **Checkpoints** | Problems are woven directly into the text — as a concept is explained, you immediately test it | You build atomic skills in real-time, not as an afterthought |
| **The Culmination** | Multi-step synthesis problems that combine everything from the chapter | You prove you can handle real exam pressure |

### The Story-Gauntlet (Problem Solving)

Each problem set is framed as a continuous real-world scenario — a lab experiment gone wrong, an engineer's dilemma, a historical discovery. The story poses an initial problem. Once solved, the story *evolves*, escalating in difficulty. All escalation questions are hidden inside clean collapsible `<details>` tags, so the reading experience stays elegant while the practice volume stays brutal.
