## How This Book Works

### The 6-Stage Method (Per Chapter)

| Stage | What You Do | Why It Works |
|-------|-------------|--------------|
| 🎯 **Stage 1: The Core Idea** | Read a 1-page explanation with a real-world analogy | You understand *what* the concept means, not just its formula |
| 🔬 **Stage 2: The Formula Lab** | See where the formula comes from, understand every variable and unit | You'll never confuse what goes in the numerator vs denominator |
| 🧱 **Stage 3: Type-wise Mastery** | 8–12 problem types, each with a solved example + 6 to 7 practice questions (with gradual increase in diffculity ) | You build *atomic skills* — one tiny pattern at a time |
| 🧱 **Stage 4: MCQ Mastery** | MCQ questions that hit the details we normally ignore along with normal style MCQ, Exampler type, jee type, boards type, ncert types| Making the student master of that concept from depth to details |
| 🔀 **Stage 5: Type Mixer** | Problems that combine 2–3 types from the same chapter | You learn to recognize which type a question belongs to |
| 📋 **Stage 6: Board Arsenal** | NCERT + Board-style questions | Clean, direct problems. You nail these first. |
| 🚀 **Stage 7: JEE Mains Arena** | Multi-step, tricky, trap-laden questions | The real test of your understanding |

### Difficulty Tags

- 🟢 **Easy** — Solve in under 1 minute
- 🟡 **Medium** — Careful thinking, 2–3 minutes
- 🔴 **Hard** — Multi-step, may involve traps, 3–5 minutes
- ⭐ **Must-Do** — This exact pattern appears repeatedly in exams
