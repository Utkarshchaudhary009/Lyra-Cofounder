## Key Formulas at a Glance

| Formula | Meaning |
|---------|---------|
| $\vec{F} = q(\vec{E} + \vec{v}\times\vec{B})$ | Lorentz Force |
| $\vec{F} = I(\vec{L}\times\vec{B})$ | Force on current-carrying conductor |
| $r = mv/(qB)$ | Radius of circular motion in $B$ |
| $T = 2\pi m/(qB)$ | Cyclotron period (independent of speed) |
| $d\vec{B} = \frac{\mu_0}{4\pi}\frac{Id\vec{l}\times\hat{r}}{r^2}$ | Biot-Savart Law |
| $B = \frac{\mu_0 I}{2R}$ | Field at center of circular loop |
| $\oint\vec{B}\cdot d\vec{l} = \mu_0 I_{enc}$ | Ampere's Circuital Law |
| $B = \mu_0 nI$ | Field inside solenoid |
| $\tau = nBIA\sin\theta$ | Torque on current loop |
| $M = IA$ | Magnetic dipole moment |
