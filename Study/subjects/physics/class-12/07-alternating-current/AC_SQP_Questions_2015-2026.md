### Case Study — Transformers (Q52–Q55, 4 marks)

> The large-scale transmission and distribution of electrical energy over long distances is done with the use of transformers. The voltage output of the generator is stepped-up. It is then transmitted over long distances to an area sub-station near the consumers. There the voltage is stepped down.

**Q52.** Which statement is true?
- (i) Energy is created when a transformer steps up the voltage
- (ii) A transformer converts AC voltage to DC voltage
- (iii) Step-up transformer increases power for transmission
- (iv) Step-down transformer decreases AC voltage

**Q53.** If the secondary coil has a greater number of turns than the primary:
- (i) Voltage is stepped-up (Vs > Vp) — step-up transformer
- (ii) Voltage is stepped-down (Vs < Vp) — step-down transformer
- (iii) Current is stepped-up (Is > Ip) — step-up transformer
- (iv) Current is stepped-down (Is < Ip) — step-down transformer

**Q54.** We step-up voltage for power transmission so that:
- (i) Current is reduced and I²R loss is cut down
- (ii) Voltage increases, power losses also increase
- (iii) Power increases before transmission
- (iv) Voltage decreases so V²/R losses reduce

**Q55.** A power transmission line feeds **2300 V** to a step-down transformer with **4000 turns** in primary. Number of turns in secondary for **230 V** output:
- (i) 4 &nbsp; (ii) 40 &nbsp; (iii) 400 &nbsp; (iv) 4000
