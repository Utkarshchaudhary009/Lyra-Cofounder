# Aarav

**Role:** Core Nine / Main audience character  
**Age:** 16  
**Class:** 11th  
**Language:** Natural UP/Bihar Hinglish

## Core Identity
Aarav is an ordinary schoolboy who notices everything. He is not the loudest or the coolest, but he is the emotional center of the group. He often watches the chaos for a second before joining it.

He is curious, quietly funny, and prone to overthinking simple things. Around Shradha, his confidence drops noticeably.

## Personality
- Observant and curious.
- Quietly funny, especially in one-on-one banter.
- Slightly introverted but not shy with close friends.
- Overthinks crush moments, exams, and small social mistakes.
- Loyal to his friends.
- Sometimes gets dragged into Kabir's nonsense despite knowing better.

## Visual DNA
- Medium-brown Indian skin tone.
- Dark expressive eyes.
- Slightly messy black hair.
- Soft oval face.
- Slim, natural teenage build.
- Rectangular school glasses.
- Light school shirt, dark trousers, black shoes, slightly worn backpack.
- Relaxed posture with one backpack strap often over his shoulder.

## Behavior
He taps a pen, adjusts his glasses when nervous, looks toward a sound before reacting, and gives tiny side-glances during jokes. He laughs more with his eyes than with big expressions.

## Relationships
**Kabir:** Best friend and constant source of trouble.  
**Shradha:** Main crush. He gets awkward around her.  
**Raghav:** Respects his intelligence but finds him too serious sometimes.  
**Vihaan:** Friendly connector.  
**Ishaan:** Quiet friendship.  
**Anaya:** Good classmate and friend.  
**Meera:** Trusts her calm nature.  
**Dev:** Looks up to him on hostel matters.

## Story Function
Aarav is the viewer's eyes. His small reactions make normal school moments feel personal and nostalgic.

## Voice
Short, natural Hinglish. He rarely gives dramatic speeches.

Examples: "Abe chhod na." / "Kya kar raha hai?" / "Bhai chup." / "Sach mein?"

## Continuity Notes
Aarav is **not** the topper, class monitor, or loud comic character. Shradha is the main crush character; Anaya remains her own separate character.

## Production Rule
Keep face, glasses, hairstyle, body proportions, uniform base, and backpack consistent across every generated shot.
