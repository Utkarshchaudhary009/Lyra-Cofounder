# Kabir

**Role:** Core Nine / Chaos engine  
**Age:** 16  
**Class:** 11th  
**Language:** Natural UP/Bihar Hinglish

## Core Identity
Kabir is the friend who turns a two-second situation into a full case. Loud, shameless, confident, and permanently ready with a bad idea, he creates most of the class's comedy without trying to be a comedian.

## Personality
- High-energy and impulsive.
- Extremely social.
- Loves teasing everyone, especially Aarav.
- Thinks he can escape consequences.
- Street-smart in small everyday situations.
- Loyal underneath the bakchodi.
- Hates awkward silence and fills it with nonsense.

## Visual DNA
- Slightly taller than Aarav.
- Messy black hair.
- Expressive eyebrows and mischievous grin.
- Slim-to-average teenage build.
- Shirt often untucked or slightly loose.
- Tie loosened, sleeves sometimes rolled.
- School bag thrown over one shoulder.

## Behavior
Kabir leans across desks, steals pens, flicks paper, grabs food, whispers plans during class, and uses huge hand gestures while telling stories. He moves fast and reacts even faster.

## Relationships
**Aarav:** Best friend and favourite target.  
**Shradha:** Loves exposing Aarav in front of her.  
**Raghav:** Calls him "topper babu" and tries to drag him into nonsense.  
**Vihaan:** Chaos partnership.  
**Ishaan:** Pulls him into random ideas.  
**Anaya:** Constant roast battle.  
**Meera:** Teases her less because she can shut him down with one look.  
**Dev:** Hostel mischief partner.

## Story Function
The chaos engine. Kabir should create movement, conflict, jokes, and sudden escalation.

## Voice
Very natural school Hinglish. Quick lines, nicknames, dramatic reactions.

Examples: "Abe sun!" / "Bhai scene ho gaya." / "Tu pagal hai kya?" / "Kasam se ek hi bite."

## Continuity Notes
Kabir is **not secretly the smartest person in class**. His intelligence is practical and social. He should stay chaotic even when things matter.

## Production Rule
Keep his messy hair, loose tie, expressive face, energetic posture, and fast physical acting consistent.
