# Raghav

**Role:** Core Nine / Topper  
**Age:** 16  
**Class:** 11th  
**Language:** Natural UP/Bihar Hinglish

## Core Identity
Raghav is the class topper who actually understands things. He is disciplined, competitive, and neat, but not a robot. He secretly enjoys the class madness and gets dragged into it more often than he admits.

## Personality
- Very intelligent and prepared.
- Competitive without being cruel.
- Calm under pressure.
- Occasionally smug when he knows the answer.
- Secretly enjoys Kabir's chaos.
- Gets genuinely annoyed when people waste time before exams.

## Visual DNA
- Neatly combed black hair.
- Rectangular or simple school glasses optional but consistent once chosen.
- Straight posture.
- Clean, perfectly tucked uniform.
- Simple watch.
- Organized backpack and pencil case.

## Behavior
He aligns his pens, opens the correct notebook before the teacher asks, keeps his worksheets together, and raises one eyebrow when the others do something stupid. When he finally laughs, it feels earned.

## Relationships
**Aarav:** Friendly and quietly respectful.  
**Kabir:** Constantly irritated, secretly entertained.  
**Shradha:** Academic equal; casual competition.  
**Vihaan:** Useful source of social information.  
**Ishaan:** Respects his creativity.  
**Anaya:** Comfortable academic friend.  
**Meera:** Trusts her judgement.  
**Dev:** Unexpected mutual respect.

## Story Function
The straight-man counterweight to Kabir. He makes jokes funnier because he reacts seriously.

## Voice
Precise but still natural Hinglish. No long lectures unless the joke needs one.

Examples: "Bhai, padh le." / "Ye itna bhi hard nahi hai." / "Preparation." / "Tum log pagal ho."

## Continuity Notes
Raghav is a topper, **not a bookworm stereotype**. He should still play, joke, eat tiffin, and participate in normal school life.

## Production Rule
Keep his neat hair, clean uniform, watch, organized props, and composed body language consistent.
