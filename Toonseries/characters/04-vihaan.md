# Vihaan

**Role:** Core Nine / Social connector  
**Age:** 17  
**Class:** 11th  
**Language:** Natural UP/Bihar Hinglish

## Core Identity
Vihaan knows everyone and somehow knows every piece of school gossip. He is confident, charming, and socially quick. He can walk into any group and leave with three new friends.

## Personality
- Charismatic and relaxed.
- Socially intelligent.
- Loves gossip more than he admits.
- Good at reading people.
- Hates being ignored.
- Likes making dramatic entrances.

## Visual DNA
- Slightly taller than most of the group.
- Distinctive but believable school hairstyle.
- Relaxed shoulders and confident walk.
- Uniform neat but casually worn.
- Water bottle or small sports item often nearby.

## Behavior
He leans against desks, talks while walking, casually joins conversations, points across the room when telling a story, and looks around before dropping gossip.

## Relationships
**Aarav:** Friendly and easygoing.  
**Kabir:** Comedy partner.  
**Raghav:** Uses him for quick updates.  
**Shradha:** Friendly and comfortable banter.  
**Anaya:** Social equal.  
**Meera:** Includes her without making a big deal of it.  
**Ishaan:** Likes his random observations.  
**Dev:** Knows hostel stories through him.

## Story Function
The connector between different groups, locations, and pieces of information.

## Voice
Fast, confident, casual Hinglish.

Examples: "Bhai, mujhe sab pata hai." / "Scene kuch aur hai." / "Tu rehne de." / "Main tha wahan."

## Continuity Notes
Vihaan is **not the class bully or the main prankster**. His power is social information and confidence.

## Production Rule
Keep his height, relaxed posture, recognizable hairstyle, and confident facial reactions consistent.
