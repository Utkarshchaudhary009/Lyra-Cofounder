# Ishaan

**Role:** Core Nine / Dreamer and creative friend  
**Age:** 16  
**Class:** 11th  
**Language:** Natural UP/Bihar Hinglish

## Core Identity
Ishaan is the quiet creative guy who notices weird little things nobody else notices. He doodles, imagines stories, and can turn a boring classroom moment into a joke with one sketch.

## Personality
- Imaginative and curious.
- Calm and slightly absent-minded.
- Quietly funny.
- Easily distracted by ideas.
- Comfortable listening more than talking.
- Can suddenly say something very funny without changing expression.

## Visual DNA
- Wavy or curly dark hair.
- Slightly oversized but realistic uniform fit.
- Sketchbook or small drawing notebook.
- Slim natural build.
- Soft, observant eyes.

## Behavior
He doodles in margins, sketches people without warning, taps pencils, stares out windows when thinking, and sometimes answers a serious question with an oddly creative observation.

## Relationships
**Aarav:** Quiet friendship.  
**Kabir:** Gets dragged into nonsense.  
**Raghav:** Mutual respect.  
**Vihaan:** Enjoys his stories.  
**Shradha:** Friendly creative banter.  
**Anaya:** Comfortable classmate.  
**Meera:** Easy silence and mutual understanding.  
**Dev:** Likes hearing hostel stories.

## Story Function
Adds visual comedy, quieter scenes, and emotional texture.

## Voice
Few words, good timing. Natural Hinglish with unexpected punchlines.

Examples: "Ye dekh." / "Bhai, ye kya hai?" / "Maine bana diya." / "Acha hai."

## Continuity Notes
Ishaan is **not the sad loner**. He is socially connected and enjoys the group; he is simply quieter.

## Production Rule
Keep hair shape, sketchbook, relaxed posture, and understated expressions consistent.
