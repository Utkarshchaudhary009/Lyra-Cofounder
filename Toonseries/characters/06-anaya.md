# Anaya

**Role:** Core Nine / Sharp friend and class anchor  
**Age:** 16  
**Class:** 11th  
**Language:** Natural UP/Bihar Hinglish

## Core Identity
Anaya is confident, observant, and quick with a perfectly timed comeback. She is not the crush character; she is one of the people who sees through everyone's nonsense immediately.

## Personality
- Sharp and confident.
- Socially aware.
- Funny without trying too hard.
- Competitive when challenged.
- Good at reading awkward situations.
- Less impulsive than Kabir.
- Can be warm without becoming overly sentimental.

## Visual DNA
- Shoulder-length dark hair.
- Expressive eyes and strong eyebrows.
- Natural teenage proportions.
- Neat school uniform.
- Simple backpack.
- Distinctive side-eye and half-smile.

## Behavior
Anaya often watches a chaotic scene for a moment before speaking. She raises one eyebrow, crosses her arms, or gives a small smile before dropping the line that ends the argument.

## Relationships
**Aarav:** Friend, never treated as a crush.  
**Kabir:** Frequent banter and roast battles.  
**Shradha:** Close female friend; contrasting energy.  
**Raghav:** Academic friendship.  
**Vihaan:** Comfortable social banter.  
**Meera:** Close friend.  
**Ishaan:** Friendly classmate.  
**Dev:** Knows his hostel reputation.

## Story Function
The reality check. She punctures fake confidence and keeps group scenes grounded.

## Voice
Natural, short, dry Hinglish.

Examples: "Tum dono kabhi normal reh sakte ho?" / "Bas kar." / "Haan haan, bahut smart ho."

## Continuity Notes
Anaya must **not be written as Aarav's crush** after Shradha becomes the canonical crush. Her role is independent and valuable.

## Production Rule
Keep hairstyle, face shape, expression language, uniform, and body proportions consistent.
