# Meera

**Role:** Core Nine / Emotional anchor  
**Age:** 16  
**Class:** 11th  
**Language:** Natural UP/Bihar Hinglish

## Core Identity
Meera is calm, kind, quietly funny, and usually the first person to notice when someone is uncomfortable or left out. She does not seek attention, but the group feels more complete when she is there.

## Personality
- Empathetic and patient.
- Introverted but socially comfortable with close friends.
- Quietly funny.
- Notices details others miss.
- Rarely panics.
- Stronger than she first appears.

## Visual DNA
- Long dark hair.
- Soft expressive face.
- Natural teenage proportions.
- Simple school uniform.
- Minimal accessories.
- Calm eyes and gentle smile.

## Behavior
She listens while others talk, quietly adjusts a friend's notebook, shares snacks, looks toward the door when someone calls a name, and gives small reactions instead of big ones. Her smile often carries more emotion than dialogue.

## Relationships
**Aarav:** Trustworthy friend.  
**Kabir:** Tolerates his nonsense and occasionally shuts him down with one sentence.  
**Shradha:** Comfortable friendship.  
**Anaya:** Close friend.  
**Raghav:** Mutual respect.  
**Vihaan:** Friendly connection.  
**Ishaan:** Easy quiet friendship.  
**Dev:** Kind but slightly amused by his hostel stories.

## Story Function
Emotional anchor for nostalgic stories and quieter moments.

## Voice
Soft, natural, never overly dramatic.

Examples: "Rehne de." / "Tu theek hai?" / "Chal, main hoon na." / "Bas kar ab."

## Continuity Notes
Meera is **not the sad girl or background extra**. She has jokes, opinions, friendships, and agency.

## Production Rule
Keep hair length, facial softness, understated expressions, and simple styling consistent.
