# Dev

**Role:** Core Nine / Hostel legend  
**Age:** 17  
**Class:** 11th  
**Language:** Natural UP/Bihar Hinglish

## Core Identity
Dev is the older-brother type of the group and the unofficial hostel problem-solver. He knows where things are, who has a charger, when the warden walks by, and how to fix small problems without making a big deal out of them.

## Personality
- Resourceful and practical.
- Mischievous but dependable.
- Calm during chaos.
- Acts older than the others.
- Pretends nothing bothers him.
- Quietly protective of friends.

## Visual DNA
- Tallest or among the tallest students in the main cast.
- Slightly rugged youthful look.
- Messy hostel hair.
- Relaxed posture.
- School uniform in class; hoodie, track pants, or casual hostel clothes outside class.
- Worn backpack or practical hostel bag.

## Behavior
He walks with a relaxed pace, checks doors before entering, casually throws a towel over his shoulder in hostel scenes, carries kettles or random supplies, and often solves problems while everyone else is still panicking.

## Relationships
**Kabir:** Hostel mischief partner.  
**Aarav:** Treats him like a younger friend.  
**Raghav:** Unexpected mutual respect.  
**Shradha:** Friendly distance; knows her mostly through the group.  
**Vihaan:** Information exchange.  
**Ishaan:** Likes his creative observations.  
**Anaya:** Casual banter.  
**Meera:** Respects her calm nature.

## Story Function
Opens the hostel world and gives the group a practical older-brother energy.

## Voice
Low-key, dry Hinglish. He rarely overreacts.

Examples: "Chal, ho jayega." / "Pehle dekh toh sahi." / "Warden aa gaya kya?" / "Tension mat le."

## Continuity Notes
Dev is a student, **not the hostel warden**. He can know hostel rules without becoming all-powerful or unrealistically connected.

## Production Rule
Keep height, messy hair, relaxed posture, and practical clothing consistent across hostel and school scenes.
