# Sharma Sir

**Role:** Core adult character / Physics teacher  
**Age:** 40  
**Subject:** Physics  
**Language:** Natural UP/Bihar Hinglish

## Core Identity
Sharma Sir is the teacher students are slightly scared of, constantly joke about, and slowly come to respect. He is strict, sarcastic, observant, and secretly more caring than he shows.

He notices everything. The class often thinks they escaped consequences when he has already understood exactly what happened.

## Personality
- Strict but fair.
- Dry sense of humor.
- Very observant.
- Low tolerance for nonsense during teaching.
- Cares about students without becoming sentimental.
- Uses silence and eye contact as often as shouting.

## Visual DNA
- Medium height and average build.
- Slightly receding black hair with some grey.
- Rectangular glasses.
- Formal light shirt and dark trousers.
- Simple wristwatch.
- Practical leather or cloth teacher bag.
- Calm face that can switch into a very sharp stare.

## Behavior
He enters with books under one arm, puts them down carefully, adjusts his glasses, writes on the board, pauses when the class gets noisy, and slowly looks toward the source instead of immediately shouting.

## Relationships
**Aarav:** Sees his observant nature.  
**Kabir:** Eternal source of trouble; Sharma Sir knows his tricks.  
**Raghav:** Trusts his preparation.  
**Shradha:** Recognizes her intelligence without making her a teacher's pet.  
**Vihaan:** Knows he hears everything.  
**Ishaan:** Occasionally notices his drawings.  
**Anaya:** Respects her confidence.  
**Meera:** Appreciates her calmness.  
**Dev:** Sees him as responsible for hostel-age students.

## Story Function
Authority figure, comic straight man, and occasional emotional anchor when stories look back on school years.

## Voice
Short, controlled, dry Hinglish. Avoid long speeches.

Examples: "Interesting." / "Continue." / "Kabir, tum khade ho jao." / "Mujhe sab dikh raha hai."

## Continuity Notes
Sharma Sir is **not the hostel warden**. Hostel stories use a separate warden character. Sharma Sir can appear in school and occasional school-linked scenes.

## Production Rule
Keep glasses, hairline, formal shirt, watch, teacher bag, and controlled body language consistent.
