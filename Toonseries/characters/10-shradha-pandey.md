# Shradha Pandey

**Role:** Core Nine+ character / Main school crush character  
**Age:** 16  
**Class:** 11th  
**Language:** Natural UP/Bihar Hinglish

## Core Identity

Shradha Pandey is the girl everyone notices, but she never behaves like she knows it. She is extremely beautiful in a natural, everyday school-girl way, confident without showing off, and one of the sharpest students in class.

She is a topper, but she never looks like a book worm. She studies fast, understands concepts quickly, finishes work on time, and then spends the rest of the period talking, laughing, teasing friends, doodling, sharing tiffin, or getting involved in whatever bakchodi is happening.

Her intelligence feels effortless. She does not carry a mountain of books or constantly talk about marks.

## Personality

- Extremely sharp and quick-witted.
- Full of banter and never misses an easy joke.
- Confident but not arrogant.
- Friendly with everyone, but keeps her own small circle.
- Can roast Kabir without raising her voice.
- Often catches Aarav staring and deliberately makes him nervous just for fun.
- Competitive when challenged.
- Warm underneath the banter.
- Has the rare ability to switch from complete bakchodi to solving a difficult question in seconds.

## Visual DNA

- Natural Indian teenage beauty.
- Expressive dark eyes.
- Long, dark, naturally styled hair.
- Soft, memorable facial features.
- Natural youthful proportions.
- Simple school uniform, neat but never overly polished.
- Small practical accessories only.
- No glamorous styling; beauty should feel believable and effortless.
- Distinctive relaxed smile and raised-eyebrow reaction.

## Behavior

Shradha rarely sits perfectly still. She taps a pen while thinking, spins it when bored, steals a friend's pencil, leans across desks during conversations, fixes her hair casually, and laughs openly when the class gets chaotic.

When someone says something stupid, she usually gives a two-second stare before delivering a dry one-liner.

When Aarav gets nervous around her, she notices immediately and often makes the situation worse for him on purpose.

## Academic Identity

Topper without looking like one.

She usually knows the answer before anyone else, but she does not wave her hand at every question. She can finish a difficult problem quickly and then spend the next five minutes arguing with Kabir about something completely useless.

Her notebooks are neat enough to be useful, but not perfect. Her desk can still have a water bottle, half-eaten snack, doodle, and random paper lying around.

## Relationship Dynamics

**Aarav:** Main crush dynamic. Shradha knows he gets nervous around her. She finds it cute and occasionally teases him, but she is never cruel.

**Kabir:** Constant banter. Kabir tries to expose Aarav. Shradha often joins the roast instead of saving him.

**Raghav:** Academic equal. They compete casually and often solve the same question in different ways.

**Anaya:** Close female friend. Anaya is more controlled; Shradha is more playful. They can exchange one look and understand what is happening.

**Meera:** Comfortable friendship. Shradha brings Meera into group conversations without forcing her.

## Story Function

Shradha should never exist only as "the pretty girl" or "the crush."

She has her own jokes, friendships, academic ability, mistakes, insecurities, ambitions, and ordinary school memories.

The crush dynamic is one thread inside a larger character.

## Signature Lines / Voice

Keep dialogue natural, short and UP/Bihar-school Hinglish.

Examples:

- "Abe tu serious hai kya?"
- "Itna bhi dimaag mat laga."
- "Haan bhai topper hoon, calculator nahi."
- "Kabir, tum chup reh sakte ho do minute?"
- "Aarav, tu mujhe dekh raha hai ya board ko?"
- "Bas kar, drama mat kar."

## Production Rule

Shradha's face, hairstyle, proportions, uniform base, and visual identity must remain consistent across all generated images and videos. Keep her beautiful but grounded, expressive but not glamorized, and unmistakably part of the same Indian school world as the rest of the cast.
