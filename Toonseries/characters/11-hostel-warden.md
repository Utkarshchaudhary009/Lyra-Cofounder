# Character 11 — Hostel Warden

**Name:** Mishra Sir
**Age:** 45–50
**Role:** Boys' hostel warden
**Home Region:** Uttar Pradesh / Bihar cultural background
**Type:** Recurring adult character

## Core Personality

Strict, sharp-eyed, practical and permanently suspicious of hostel boys. He has a dry sense of humor and secretly cares about the students. He doesn't need to shout often; one look is usually enough.

He knows every hostel trick because he has seen it all before.

## Visual Identity

- Medium-to-stocky build
- Thick moustache
- Short neatly combed black-and-grey hair
- Simple spectacles
- Usually wears a tucked-in shirt and dark trousers
- Carries a key ring and a small register
- Comfortable slippers or simple shoes
- Slightly tired but alert expression

## Signature Behaviors

- Suddenly appears when students think the corridor is safe
- Stops outside rooms during rounds and listens before entering
- Taps his register against his palm when annoyed
- Gives long silent looks instead of immediately scolding
- Knows exactly which student is responsible for a mess

## Comedy Engine

The boys treat avoiding Mishra Sir like a military operation. Mishra Sir usually knows what they are doing before they do it.

**Typical line:**
> "Beta, mujhe sab pata hai. Tum log bas acting achhi kar lete ho."

## Emotional Side

He genuinely worries about homesick students, sick students and boys who are struggling away from home. His care is usually shown through actions rather than speeches.

## Relationships

- **Dev:** Frequent troublemaker; Mishra Sir knows Dev's tricks.
- **Kabir:** His biggest headache.
- **Aarav:** Generally trusts him.
- **Raghav:** Considers him responsible.
- **Hostel boys:** Strict authority figure who gradually becomes a familiar part of their growing-up years.

## AI Continuity Rules

Keep Mishra Sir visually consistent across every hostel episode. He is the **hostel warden**, not Sharma Sir. Never merge their identities.
