# Character 12 — Aarav's Sister

**Name:** Nandini
**Age:** 19
**Role:** Aarav's older sister
**Type:** Recurring family character

## Core Personality

Sharp-tongued, funny, caring and extremely good at getting under Aarav's skin. She acts like she is constantly annoyed with him, but she is also the first person to defend him when someone else crosses a line.

Their relationship is built on effortless sibling banter rather than sentimental speeches.

## Visual Identity

- 19-year-old Indian college student
- Dark expressive eyes
- Long dark hair, usually tied casually at home
- Natural everyday appearance
- Comfortable home clothes: simple kurta, T-shirt, track pants or casual college wear
- Usually carries a phone, notebook or hair tie
- Relaxed posture at home

## Signature Behaviors

- Steals food from Aarav's plate while claiming she doesn't want it
- Calls him by his full name when he has done something stupid
- Teases him about school and friends
- Borrows his things without asking
- Pretends not to care, then quietly helps him
- Keeps old childhood photos and occasionally uses them as blackmail

## Comedy Engine

Nandini knows exactly which button to press to annoy Aarav.

**Typical banter:**

**AARAV:**  
Mummy se bol dunga.

**NANDINI:**  
Haan bol. Main bhi bolungi tu kal mera charger le gaya tha.

**AARAV:**  
Woh toh bas do minute ke liye liya tha.

**NANDINI:**  
Teen din.

## Emotional Side

Under all the teasing, Nandini and Aarav have a strong sibling bond. Their affection is shown through tiny ordinary moments: saving him the last piece of his favorite food, fixing something for him, sitting together during family festivals, looking through childhood photos, or quietly checking whether he is okay.

She should never become overly sentimental without a natural reason. The emotion comes from familiarity and shared history.

## Relationships

- **Aarav:** Younger brother; constant banter, occasional fights, deep trust.
- **Parents:** Comfortable family dynamic; she often acts like a second authority figure toward Aarav.
- **Kabir:** She knows enough about him to embarrass Aarav.

## Story Function

Nandini opens the universe beyond school and hostel. She is especially useful for festival episodes, home scenes, childhood flashbacks and stories about growing up.

## AI Continuity Rules

Keep Nandini visually distinct from the school characters. Her home scenes should feel warmer, more intimate and less performative than school scenes. Do not turn her into a generic "perfect sister"; her teasing and occasional selfishness are essential to making the sibling relationship feel real.
