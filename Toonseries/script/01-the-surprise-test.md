# The Surprise Test

**Type:** Reel
**Format:** YouTube Short / Animated Reel
**Estimated Duration:** 45 seconds
**Series:** Toonseries

## Premise

A completely normal school afternoon turns into instant panic when Sharma Sir announces a surprise test.

## Script

### Scene 1 — Classroom — Afternoon

The classroom is noisy. Students are talking. Aarav is doodling in the corner of his notebook.

Kabir leans over.

**KABIR:**  
Bro… homework kiya?

Aarav slowly looks at him.

**AARAV:**  
Which homework?

Kabir smiles.

**KABIR:**  
Exactly.

They quietly laugh.

### Scene 2 — Classroom

The door opens.

Sharma Sir walks in carrying a stack of papers.

The entire classroom gradually becomes silent.

He puts the papers on the desk.

Aarav looks at Kabir.

Kabir looks at Aarav.

Neither says anything.

Sharma Sir turns toward the class.

**SHARMA SIR:**  
Take out a sheet of paper.

Silence.

Someone in the back whispers:

**STUDENT:**  
Bhai...

### Scene 3 — Rapid Cuts

Aarav searches his bag. Nothing.

Kabir searches his bag. Nothing.

Raghav calmly takes out a perfectly folded sheet of paper.

Kabir stares at him.

**KABIR:**  
Tu insaan hai?

Raghav doesn't even look up.

**RAGHAV:**  
Preparation.

### Scene 4 — Classroom

Sharma Sir starts distributing the papers.

Kabir leans toward Aarav.

**KABIR:**  
Question kitne hain?

Aarav looks at the paper. His expression drops.

**AARAV:**  
...Five.

Kabir looks relieved.

**KABIR:**  
Bas?

Aarav turns the page around.

**AARAV:**  
Five pages.

Kabir's smile disappears.

**CUT TO BLACK.**

### End Card

> School mein sabse dangerous sentence...
>
> **"Take out a sheet of paper."**
