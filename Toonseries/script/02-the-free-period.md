# The Free Period

**Type:** Reel  
**Format:** YouTube Short / Animated Reel  
**Duration:** 40 seconds  
**Structure:** 4 scenes × 10 seconds  
**Language:** Natural UP/Bihar school Hinglish  
**Series:** Toonseries

## Premise

Teacher leaves for two minutes. Class ko lagta hai azaadi mil gayi. Within seconds, pura classroom apna asli roop dikha deta hai.

## Scene 1 — 0:00–0:10 — Do Minute Ki Azaadi

Sharma Sir writes a long equation on the board. Kabir is almost asleep on the bench. Aarav keeps checking the clock. Ishaan draws tiny cartoons in the corner of his notebook. Raghav is actually solving the question. Sharma Sir puts down the chalk, picks up his register and turns around.

**SHARMA SIR:**  
Main do minute mein aata hoon. Koi bahar nahi jayega.

He walks out and closes the door.

Silence.

Kabir waits one second. Then two. He slowly stands on the bench.

**KABIR:**  
Bhai... free period.

Aarav looks at him.

**AARAV:**  
Do minute ke liye.

Kabir opens both arms like he just won a match.

**KABIR:**  
Do minute mein aadmi zindagi badal deta hai.

## Scene 2 — 0:10–0:20 — Pura Kalesh

The classroom explodes. Benches move, students turn around, someone starts beating a desk like a drum, one boy throws a paper ball, Vihaan starts talking to three different groups at once. Ishaan runs to the board and draws a terrible moustache on the equation. Kabir grabs Aarav's water bottle and takes a sip without asking.

**AARAV:**  
Abe meri bottle!

**KABIR:**  
Apni hi samajh ke pi raha hoon.

Anaya turns around from her seat.

**ANAYA:**  
Tum log paida hi sharafat ke against hue the kya?

Kabir points at the whole room.

**KABIR:**  
Didi, hum toh sabse seedhe hain. Dekho baaki log.

Behind him, a paper plane hits the fan and falls onto Raghav's desk.

Raghav doesn't even look up.

**RAGHAV:**  
Pagal ho tum log.

## Scene 3 — 0:20–0:30 — “Guys...”

Meera is at the back row. She notices the corridor shadow moving past the glass panel. She stops smiling and looks toward the door. Nobody notices. Kabir is now trying to balance a ruler on his nose. Vihaan is telling a story with huge hand gestures. Ishaan adds another stupid drawing to the board.

Meera quietly says:

**MEERA:**  
Guys...

Nobody stops.

She looks at the door again.

**MEERA:**  
Guys.

Kabir keeps laughing.

Meera points at the door and whispers:

**MEERA:**  
Sir.

Everything dies instantly.

The paper ball freezes in someone's hand. Ishaan drops the chalk. Kabir jumps off the bench and lands straight on his chair. Everyone starts fixing their hair and shirts at the same time.

## Scene 4 — 0:30–0:40 — Sir Sab Dekh Raha Tha

The door slowly opens. Sharma Sir stands there with the register in his hand. The whole class is frozen in ridiculous positions. Kabir is still half-standing, Ishaan has chalk on his fingers, Vihaan is turned around, Anaya is trying not to laugh, Raghav is somehow still writing.

Sharma Sir looks around slowly.

Long silence.

**SHARMA SIR:**  
Ho gaya free period?

Nobody answers.

He walks to his desk, puts down the register and picks up the chalk.

**SHARMA SIR:**  
Chalo. Ab dekhta hoon kiski awaaz pehle niklegi.

Everyone sits straight.

Kabir leans toward Aarav and whispers:

**KABIR:**  
Bhai... sir sab dekh raha tha kya?

Aarav watches Sharma Sir writing on the board.

**AARAV:**  
Sir se kuch nahi chhupta, be.

Kabir slowly nods.

**KABIR:**  
Mushkil aadmi hai.

**CUT TO BLACK.**
