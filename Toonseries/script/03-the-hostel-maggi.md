# The Hostel Maggi

**Type:** Reel  
**Format:** YouTube Short / Animated Reel  
**Duration:** 40 seconds  
**Structure:** 4 scenes × 10 seconds  
**Language:** Natural UP/Bihar hostel Hinglish  
**Series:** Toonseries

## Premise

Raat ke hostel mein Maggi ki craving lagi hai. Do packet Maggi, teen pagal ladke, aur corridor mein ghoomta warden. Bas, operation shuru.

## Scene 1 — 0:00–0:10 — Maggi Ka Jugaad

Hostel room at 10:47 PM. Dev lies on his bed scrolling an old magazine, then suddenly sits up hungry. He checks his cupboard, opens his friend's drawer, lifts a pillow, finds nothing. Kabir is lying on the other bed with a book covering his face. Dev looks at him.

**DEV:**  
Bhai... bhook lag rahi hai.

Kabir doesn't move.

**KABIR:**  
Toh?

**DEV:**  
Maggi hai?

Kabir slowly removes the book from his face and looks around like a criminal. He reaches inside an old textbook and pulls out two packets.

**KABIR:**  
Emergency maal hai.

Aarav looks up from his bed.

**AARAV:**  
Kitab mein Maggi?

**KABIR:**  
Padhai humse na hui. Kaam toh aa rahi hai.

Dev grabs the packets. They all get ready to leave.

## Scene 2 — 0:10–0:20 — Operation Maggi

The boys sneak through the hostel corridor. Dev carries the kettle like it's some secret weapon. Kabir hides the Maggi packets inside his shirt. Aarav walks ahead and keeps checking both sides. They hear a door open and immediately press themselves against the wall. A junior walks past, sees them, pauses, then shakes his head and leaves.

**AARAV:**  
Hum log chori karne ja rahe hain kya?

**DEV:**  
Abe Maggi hai. Itna serious mat ho.

Kabir pulls the packet halfway out.

**KABIR:**  
Bas awaaz mat karna.

The packet makes a loud **CRINKLE**.

All three stare at him.

**DEV:**  
Wah re ninja.

They reach the pantry. Dev quietly opens the door.

## Scene 3 — 0:20–0:30 — Warden Ka Bhoot

Inside the pantry, Dev fills the kettle while Kabir tears open the Maggi packet. Aarav guards the door. Suddenly footsteps echo down the corridor. Everyone freezes. Dev kills the light. Kabir hides the noodles behind his back. Aarav peeks through the doorway.

**AARAV:**  
Kaun hai?

Vihaan suddenly appears from the dark.

**VIHAAN:**  
Warden.

Everyone nearly jumps.

**KABIR:**  
Kahan?!

Vihaan points upstairs.

**VIHAAN:**  
Second floor.

Beat.

**VIHAAN:**  
Hum first floor pe hain, pagal.

Dev stares at him.

**DEV:**  
Toh dara kyun raha hai be?

Vihaan grins.

**VIHAAN:**  
Maza aa raha tha.

Kabir shakes his head and starts cooking.

**KABIR:**  
Pagal aadmi hai.

## Scene 4 — 0:30–0:40 — Asli Wala Khel

The Maggi cooks. Steam rises. Everyone leans closer as the smell fills the little pantry. Dev takes a spoon and tastes it. Kabir grabs a bowl. Aarav laughs. Then a calm voice comes from directly behind them.

**WARDEN:**  
Bahut badiya khushboo aa rahi hai.

All three freeze.

Kabir slowly lowers the bowl.

The warden steps into frame. He looks at the Maggi, then at the boys.

**WARDEN:**  
Mere liye hai?

Silence.

Dev looks at Kabir.

Kabir looks at the two packets, then at the warden.

**KABIR:**  
Ji... aapke liye hi bana rahe the.

Warden sits down casually.

**WARDEN:**  
Mirchi kam hai. Do aur packet daal do.

The boys look at each other in disbelief.

**DEV:**  
Sir bhi apne hi type ke nikle.

Warden gives him a look.

**DEV:**  
Mera matlab... sir best hain.

Everyone starts laughing as the warden takes the bowl.

**CUT TO BLACK.**
