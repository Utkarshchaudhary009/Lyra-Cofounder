# The Monitor

**Type:** Reel  
**Format:** YouTube Short / Animated Reel  
**Estimated Duration:** 40 seconds  
**Series:** Toonseries  
**Language:** UP/Bihar Hinglish  
**Structure:** 4 scenes × 10 seconds

## Premise

The class monitor takes his duty way too seriously. One tiny classroom fight turns into a full desi courtroom, complete with witnesses, fake evidence, and one very confused teacher.

---

## Scene 1 — 0:00–0:10 — The Crime

**Location:** Classroom, just before the teacher arrives.

The class is noisy. Kabir is sitting on Aarav's desk, eating chips. Aarav tries to grab the packet. Kabir pulls it away. They tug at it from both sides.

The packet suddenly tears.

Chips fly all over the floor.

Both boys freeze.

Raghav looks down at the mess and immediately starts picking up the chips.

**KABIR:**  
Arey bhai! Tu kheench kyun raha tha?

**AARAV:**  
Main? Tu packet chhod nahi raha tha!

Vihaan points at them like a news reporter.

**VIHAAN:**  
Ho gaya. Aaj FIR kategi.

---

## Scene 2 — 0:10–0:20 — Monitor Mode ON

The class monitor, Anaya, walks over with a notebook in her hand.

She looks at the chips on the floor, then at Kabir and Aarav.

She slowly opens the notebook.

**ANAYA:**  
Naam batao.

Kabir stares at her.

**KABIR:**  
Kya?

**ANAYA:**  
Naam. Dono ka.

Aarav starts laughing.

**AARAV:**  
Pagal ho gayi hai kya?

Anaya writes something anyway.

**ANAYA:**  
Theek hai. 'Dono pagal'.

Kabir tries to peek into the notebook.

**KABIR:**  
Oye, mera naam spelling sahi likhna.

---

## Scene 3 — 0:20–0:30 — Full Investigation

Anaya makes Kabir and Aarav stand beside the desks.

Meera watches while Ishaan draws a tiny courtroom scene in his notebook.

Vihaan immediately becomes a fake witness and raises his hand.

**VIHAAN:**  
Ma'am, main sab dekha hoon.

Anaya turns toward him.

**ANAYA:**  
Tu wahan tha hi nahi.

Vihaan points confidently toward the back bench.

**VIHAAN:**  
Body wahan nahi tha. Nazar toh thi.

Everyone starts laughing.

Raghav quietly holds up the torn chips packet.

**RAGHAV:**  
Evidence.

Kabir looks offended.

**KABIR:**  
Bhai tu dost hai ya police?

---

## Scene 4 — 0:30–0:40 — Teacher Arrives

The classroom door suddenly opens.

Sharma Sir walks in.

Everyone instantly sits down.

Anaya closes her notebook.

Kabir quickly kicks the chips under Aarav's desk.

Sharma Sir looks around at the silent class.

Then he notices the chips on the floor.

**SHARMA SIR:**  
Kya chal raha tha?

Nobody speaks.

Sharma Sir looks directly at Kabir.

**SHARMA SIR:**  
Kabir?

Kabir points at Aarav without hesitation.

**KABIR:**  
Sir, main toh bechara innocent hoon.

Aarav looks at him in disbelief.

**AARAV:**  
Abe saale—

Sharma Sir raises an eyebrow.

**SHARMA SIR:**  
Achha. Dono khade ho jao.

Kabir whispers to Aarav as they stand.

**KABIR:**  
Bhai, monitor ko kabhi chips mat dikhana.

**CUT TO BLACK.**

### End Card

> **School mein monitor banna = chhota sa power milna, phir full MLA banna.**
