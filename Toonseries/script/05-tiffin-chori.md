# Tiffin Chori

**Type:** Reel  
**Format:** YouTube Short / Animated Reel  
**Estimated Duration:** 40 seconds  
**Series:** Toonseries  
**Language:** UP/Bihar Hinglish  
**Structure:** 4 scenes × 10 seconds

## Premise

Aarav brings the legendary aloo paratha from home. Kabir says he only wants one bite. Ten seconds later, the whole class is involved in a ridiculous investigation over one missing paratha.

---

## Scene 1 — 0:00–0:10 — The Tiffin Opens

**Location:** Classroom, lunch break.

The bell rings. Everyone instantly opens their tiffins.

Aarav slowly opens his steel tiffin. The smell of hot aloo paratha spreads.

Kabir's head turns from three benches away.

**KABIR:**  
Abe ohooo... kya maal laya hai?

Aarav pulls out a paratha and starts eating.

Kabir slides his chair closer.

**KABIR:**  
Bas ek bite de na, kasam se ek hi.

**AARAV:**  
Nahi.

Kabir puts a hand on his chest.

**KABIR:**  
Dost ke liye itna bhi nahi?

Aarav takes another huge bite just to annoy him.

Kabir stares at the paratha like he has been personally betrayed.

---

## Scene 2 — 0:10–0:20 — The Distraction

Kabir suddenly points toward the classroom door.

**KABIR:**  
Abe Sharma Sir!

Aarav instantly turns around.

Kabir quickly grabs the paratha and stuffs it into his mouth.

Aarav turns back.

The paratha is gone.

**AARAV:**  
ABE!

Kabir chews innocently, shaking his head.

**KABIR:**  
Kya hua?

Aarav checks the tiffin, then Kabir's face.

**AARAV:**  
Mera paratha kahan hai?

Kabir points at the floor.

**KABIR:**  
Gir gaya hoga.

Aarav looks under the desk.

Nothing.

---

## Scene 3 — 0:20–0:30 — CID Begins

Aarav stands up and looks around.

**AARAV:**  
Kisi ne mera paratha uthaya hai.

Everyone suddenly becomes interested.

Vihaan leans back like a detective.

**VIHAAN:**  
Sab chup. Crime scene hai.

Ishaan points toward Kabir.

**ISHAAN:**  
Iske muh pe aloo laga hai.

Everyone looks at Kabir.

Kabir wipes his mouth quickly.

**KABIR:**  
Arey chutney hai!

Anaya folds her arms.

**ANAYA:**  
Chutney? Brown aloo chutney?

Raghav checks the empty tiffin.

**RAGHAV:**  
Motive bhi hai. Bhookha tha.

Kabir looks around.

**KABIR:**  
Tum log dost ho ya police station?

---

## Scene 4 — 0:30–0:40 — The Real Culprit

Everyone searches around the classroom.

Meera suddenly points toward the window.

A stray school dog is sitting outside with a piece of paratha near its paws.

Everyone goes silent.

Kabir looks relieved.

**KABIR:**  
Dekha! Bola tha na gir gaya hoga.

Aarav slowly turns toward him.

**AARAV:**  
Haan. Aur ye tere muh pe aloo kaise aaya?

Kabir freezes.

The whole group starts laughing.

Kabir quickly grabs Aarav's remaining paratha.

**KABIR:**  
Chal, ab jo bacha hai woh toh de.

Aarav pulls the tiffin away.

**AARAV:**  
Abey bhaag!

Kabir runs. Aarav chases him around the desks while everyone laughs.

**CUT TO BLACK.**

### End Card

> **School mein tiffin share nahi hota tha... loot hota tha.**
