# Last Bench

**Type:** Reel  
**Format:** YouTube Short / Animated Reel  
**Estimated Duration:** 40 seconds  
**Series:** Toonseries  
**Language:** UP/Bihar Hinglish  
**Structure:** 4 scenes × 10 seconds

## Premise

Aarav finally gets moved to the last bench with Kabir. What starts as the best news ever quickly turns into the most dangerous place in the classroom when Sharma Sir notices exactly what happens there.

---

## Scene 1 — 0:00–0:10 — Promotion

**Location:** Classroom, morning.

Sharma Sir points toward Aarav's seat.

**SHARMA SIR:**  
Aarav, uth. Last bench pe jao.

Aarav looks confused.

Kabir immediately starts smiling.

**KABIR:**  
Aaja bhai. Aaj se asli life shuru.

Aarav picks up his bag and walks toward the back.

Kabir dramatically clears half the bench for him.

**KABIR:**  
Welcome to VIP section.

Aarav sits down.

He looks around at the back row like he has entered a new world.

**AARAV:**  
Yahan se board dikhta bhi hai?

Kabir squints toward the blackboard.

**KABIR:**  
Board nahi dikhta. Life dikhti hai.

---

## Scene 2 — 0:10–0:20 — Back Bench Technology

The lesson starts.

Sharma Sir writes on the board.

Kabir slowly pulls out a tiny folded paper from inside his notebook.

Aarav looks at it.

**AARAV:**  
Ye kya hai?

**KABIR:**  
Emergency notes.

Aarav opens it.

It's completely blank.

**AARAV:**  
Isme kuch hai hi nahi.

Kabir whispers proudly.

**KABIR:**  
Exactly. Teacher ko lagega notes hain.

Aarav tries not to laugh.

Raghav turns around from the front row.

**RAGHAV:**  
Tum dono pagal ho.

Kabir whispers back.

**KABIR:**  
Tu topper hai, tujhe samajh nahi aayega.

---

## Scene 3 — 0:20–0:30 — The Danger Zone

Kabir starts flicking tiny bits of paper toward Vihaan.

Vihaan catches one and throws it back.

It hits Ishaan.

Ishaan looks around, confused.

He picks it up and immediately throws it toward Kabir.

Kabir ducks.

The paper flies past him and lands directly on Sharma Sir's desk.

The entire class freezes.

Sharma Sir slowly picks it up.

He unfolds it.

Written on it:

**"Kabir pagal hai."**

Sharma Sir looks toward the back bench.

Kabir points at Aarav.

Aarav's eyes go wide.

**AARAV:**  
Abe kya kar raha hai!

Kabir whispers:

**KABIR:**  
Bhai teamwork.

---

## Scene 4 — 0:30–0:40 — Reality Check

Sharma Sir walks slowly toward the last bench.

Kabir tries to hide the paper under his notebook.

Aarav sits perfectly straight, pretending to study.

Sharma Sir stops beside them.

He looks at the paper, then at Kabir.

**SHARMA SIR:**  
Last bench pe bahut maza aa raha hai?

Kabir gives a nervous smile.

**KABIR:**  
Sir... thoda.

Sharma Sir points toward the first bench.

**SHARMA SIR:**  
Dono uthao apna samaan.

Kabir's face falls.

Aarav picks up his bag.

As they walk away, Kabir whispers:

**KABIR:**  
VIP se general kar diya.

Aarav laughs.

**CUT TO BLACK.**

### End Card

> **Har school mein last bench ek jagah nahi tha... emotion tha.**
