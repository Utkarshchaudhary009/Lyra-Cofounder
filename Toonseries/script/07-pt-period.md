# PT Period

**Type:** Reel  
**Format:** YouTube Short / Animated Reel  
**Estimated Duration:** 40 seconds  
**Series:** Toonseries  
**Language:** UP/Bihar Hinglish  
**Structure:** 4 scenes × 10 seconds

## Premise

The one period everyone waits for—PT. But just when the class is ready to run outside, the PT teacher announces something nobody wanted to hear.

---

## Scene 1 — 0:00–0:10 — Best News Ever

**Location:** Classroom, right before PT period.

The classroom is dead quiet. Everyone is staring at the clock.

The second hand moves.

The bell rings.

The whole class explodes out of their seats.

Kabir jumps up and grabs his bag.

**KABIR:**  
PT! Bhai, aaj toh cricket pakka!

Vihaan grabs a tennis ball from his desk.

**VIHAAN:**  
Aaj Sharma Sir ka naam bhi bhool jayenge.

Raghav slowly closes his notebook.

**RAGHAV:**  
Finally.

Aarav runs toward the door with everyone else.

Then the classroom door opens.

The PT teacher stands there holding a register.

Everyone stops.

---

## Scene 2 — 0:10–0:20 — The Betrayal

The PT teacher looks at the excited class.

**PT TEACHER:**  
Ground nahi jaana.

The smiles disappear one by one.

Kabir slowly lowers his bag.

**KABIR:**  
Kyun, sir?

**PT TEACHER:**  
Aaj test hai.

Silence.

Vihaan looks at Aarav in disbelief.

**VIHAAN:**  
PT mein bhi test?

Raghav whispers:

**RAGHAV:**  
Written test hoga?

The teacher points toward the corridor.

**PT TEACHER:**  
Sab ground pe line lagao.

Kabir looks relieved for half a second.

Then the teacher continues.

**PT TEACHER:**  
Running test.

Kabir's face drops again.

---

## Scene 3 — 0:20–0:30 — Olympic Training

The class reaches the ground.

Students line up for running.

Kabir stretches dramatically, touching his toes and shaking his legs.

**AARAV:**  
Tu Olympic mein jaa raha hai kya?

**KABIR:**  
Aaj jaan laga denge.

Whistle blows.

Everyone runs.

Kabir sprints ahead for two seconds, then immediately slows down.

**KABIR:**  
Abe ruk! Starting mein itna tez kaun bhaagta hai!

Aarav runs past him.

**AARAV:**  
Tu hi toh bol raha tha jaan laga denge!

Kabir bends down, holding his knees.

**KABIR:**  
Jaan bachana bhi zaroori hai, bhai.

---

## Scene 4 — 0:30–0:40 — The Real PT

The teacher blows the whistle again.

Everyone thinks the run is over.

They start walking toward the water taps.

**PT TEACHER:**  
Next round!

The entire class turns around in horror.

Kabir looks at Aarav.

**KABIR:**  
Bhai, school badal lete hain.

Aarav laughs while trying to catch his breath.

Vihaan suddenly points toward the cricket ground.

**VIHAAN:**  
Ball mil gaya toh bhaagenge nahi.

Kabir immediately starts running toward the cricket field.

**KABIR:**  
Cricket ke liye jaan hazir hai!

Everyone chases him.

The PT teacher shouts from behind:

**PT TEACHER:**  
Oye! Line mein!

Everyone freezes.

**CUT TO BLACK.**

### End Card

> **PT period ka asli dukh: period PT ka ho, kaam PT wala na ho.**
