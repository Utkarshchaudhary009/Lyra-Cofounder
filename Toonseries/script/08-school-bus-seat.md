# School Bus Seat

**Type:** Reel  
**Format:** YouTube Short / Animated Reel  
**Estimated Duration:** 40 seconds  
**Series:** Toonseries  
**Language:** UP/Bihar Hinglish  
**Structure:** 4 scenes × 10 seconds

## Premise

In school bus life, a seat is not a seat. It is property. Aarav reaches late, and Kabir has already started a full legal battle to save their usual window seat.

---

## Scene 1 — 0:00–0:10 — Seat Pe Kabza

**Location:** School bus, morning. Rainy sky, noisy students, bags in the aisle, bus horn outside.

Aarav runs toward the bus, backpack bouncing, while the driver taps the horn.

He climbs in and scans the crowded bus.

His usual window seat is visible.

Kabir is already sitting there, pressed against the window with his bag spread across the other seat.

Aarav points at the bag.

**AARAV:**  
Abe hata na! Ye meri seat hai.

Kabir pulls the bag closer like security staff.

**KABIR:**  
Aaj se nahi. Aaj se ye occupied hai.

Aarav grabs the bag.

Kabir grabs it back.

The bus jerks forward and both nearly fall into the aisle.

**KABIR:**  
Dekha? Seat bhi mere saath hai.

---

## Scene 2 — 0:10–0:20 — Full Court Case

Aarav stands beside the seat while the bus bumps over a speed breaker.

Vihaan turns around from the front and notices the fight.

**VIHAAN:**  
Kya kand hai?

Aarav points at Kabir.

**AARAV:**  
Roz isi pe baithte hain hum.

Kabir immediately raises one finger.

**KABIR:**  
Correction. Hum nahi. Main.

Meera laughs from the next row.

Anaya looks over the seat.

**ANAYA:**  
Kal kaun baitha tha?

Kabir answers instantly.

**KABIR:**  
Main.

Aarav protests.

**AARAV:**  
Kal toh school hi nahi aaya tha tu!

Kabir pauses.

The whole row bursts out laughing.

Kabir points at Aarav.

**KABIR:**  
Details mein mat ja.

---

## Scene 3 — 0:20–0:30 — Jugaad

The bus turns sharply. Aarav spots an empty half-seat behind Kabir.

He quickly squeezes in, then stretches one hand through the gap and starts pulling the curtain aside to get the window view.

Kabir blocks it with his elbow.

Aarav pushes back.

Both keep fighting silently while the bus shakes.

Raghav looks up from his book.

**RAGHAV:**  
Tum dono seat ke liye lad rahe ho ya shaadi kar rahe ho?

Everyone laughs.

Aarav suddenly pulls the curtain open.

A burst of cool morning air hits both faces.

Kabir's hair flies everywhere.

Kabir fixes his hair and looks offended.

**KABIR:**  
Bhai, dignity naam ki bhi cheez hoti hai.

Aarav smiles.

**AARAV:**  
Window meri.

---

## Scene 4 — 0:30–0:40 — The Twist

The bus stops at a traffic light.

The driver opens the door.

The school van conductor from the front calls out:

**CONDUCTOR:**  
Aarav! Seat change kar lo beta. Sir aa rahe hain.

Both boys freeze.

Sharma Sir climbs into the bus with a register.

Kabir instantly sits straight, removes his bag from the seat, and shifts toward the aisle.

Aarav quietly takes the window seat.

Sharma Sir walks past them.

Kabir whispers:

**KABIR:**  
Aaj tu jeet gaya.

Aarav smiles and looks outside.

The bus starts moving again.

Kabir leans over.

**KABIR:**  
Kal se phir meri.

Aarav laughs.

**CUT TO BLACK.**

### End Card

> **School bus mein friendship alag thi... seat ke time sab chor the.**
