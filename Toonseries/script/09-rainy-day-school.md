# Rainy Day School

**Type:** Reel  
**Format:** YouTube Short / Animated Reel  
**Estimated Duration:** 40 seconds  
**Series:** Toonseries  
**Language:** UP/Bihar Hinglish  
**Structure:** 4 scenes × 10 seconds

## Premise

It rains during school, and suddenly every student forgets that they are students. Wet shoes, broken umbrellas, slippery corridors, and one legendary race to get home before the rain gets worse.

---

## Scene 1 — 0:00–0:10 — Baarish Dekhte Hi

**Location:** Classroom, after lunch. Heavy rain hits the windows; thunder in the distance.

Students stop writing and turn toward the windows.

Kabir presses his face near the glass.

**KABIR:**  
Ohooo! Aaj toh school gaya.

Aarav looks outside at the rain pouring over the playground.

**AARAV:**  
Ab ghar kaise jayenge?

Vihaan stands on a bench to get a better look.

**VIHAAN:**  
Bhai, road pura talab ho gaya.

Ishaan draws a tiny boat in his notebook.

Anaya turns around.

**ANAYA:**  
Tum log padhai karoge ya weather channel banoge?

A huge thunderclap hits.

Everyone jumps.

Kabir points outside.

**KABIR:**  
Dekha? Nature bhi bol rahi hai chhutti karo.

---

## Scene 2 — 0:10–0:20 — Corridor Ka Paani

The final bell rings.

Students rush out with bags over their heads.

The corridor floor is wet. One boy slips, catches the railing, and saves himself.

Kabir steps carefully, then suddenly jumps over a puddle and lands badly.

**KABIR:**  
Aaa! Joota gaya!

Aarav grabs his shoulder and laughs.

**AARAV:**  
Bhai hero banne gaya tha na?

Kabir lifts his muddy shoe.

**KABIR:**  
Hero nahi. Aaj se kisan.

Meera walks past holding her bag above her head.

Vihaan opens an umbrella indoors, walks three steps, and immediately hits a ceiling fan.

**VIHAAN:**  
Abe yaar!

Everyone laughs.

---

## Scene 3 — 0:20–0:30 — The Great Escape

Outside the school gate, the rain is heavier.

Students crowd under the shade, waiting for buses, parents, or auto-rickshaws.

Kabir looks at the road, then at Aarav.

**KABIR:**  
Ready?

Aarav looks confused.

**AARAV:**  
Kisliye?

Kabir suddenly sprints into the rain.

**KABIR:**  
Jo ghar pehle pahuncha, woh king!

Aarav laughs and runs after him.

Vihaan joins.

Then Meera and Anaya start running too.

Bags bounce, shoes splash through puddles, umbrellas flip inside out, and everyone screams and laughs while racing down the road.

Raghav stays under the gate, completely dry.

He calls after them:

**RAGHAV:**  
Pagal ho sab!

Kabir shouts back while running:

**KABIR:**  
Haan!

---

## Scene 4 — 0:30–0:40 — Ghar Pahunch Ke

Cut to evening.

Aarav reaches home soaked, drops his bag, and removes his shoes at the door.

His mother looks at him from inside.

**MOM:**  
Chhatri kisliye di thi?

Aarav points toward the broken umbrella handle hanging from his bag.

**AARAV:**  
Kaam karte karte toot gayi.

His phone buzzes.

Kabir's message appears:

**KABIR TEXT:**  
Bhai kal bhi baarish ho toh school jayenge?

Aarav types:

**AARAV TEXT:**  
Haan. Mazaa aaya tha.

He smiles.

Outside the window, the rain keeps falling.

**CUT TO BLACK.**

### End Card

> **Baarish mein bheegna problem nahi tha... ghar jaake mummy ke saamne jaana problem tha.**
