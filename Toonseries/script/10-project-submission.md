# Project Submission

**Type:** Reel  
**Format:** YouTube Short / Animated Reel  
**Estimated Duration:** 40 seconds  
**Series:** Toonseries  
**Language:** UP/Bihar Hinglish  
**Structure:** 4 scenes × 10 seconds

## Premise

The project is due today. Nobody has done anything. By lunch break, the entire group has invented a full plan that involves stealing charts, printing at a photocopy shop, and making Raghav do the actual work.

---

## Scene 1 — 0:00–0:10 — Kal Karenge

**Location:** Classroom, first period. A school bell rings. Students dump bags beside their desks.

Raghav pulls a project file out of his bag and places it on the desk.

**RAGHAV:**  
Project complete karke laye ho na sab?

Silence.

Kabir slowly closes his notebook.

Aarav looks at Vihaan.

Vihaan looks at Ishaan.

Ishaan looks at Meera.

Meera shrugs.

**KABIR:**  
Haan bhai... almost.

Raghav opens the file.

**RAGHAV:**  
Almost kya?

Kabir taps his own chest.

**KABIR:**  
Mera confidence complete hai.

Aarav starts laughing.

Raghav puts his head down on the desk.

---

## Scene 2 — 0:10–0:20 — Emergency Meeting

The group crowds around the last bench.

Raghav throws a blank chart paper onto the desk.

**RAGHAV:**  
Forty minutes mein presentation hai.

Kabir picks up the chart and immediately starts drawing a huge title.

**KABIR:**  
Title ready.

Aarav looks at it.

It says: **"OUR PROJECT"** in giant crooked letters.

**AARAV:**  
Bas?

**KABIR:**  
Bhai foundation strong hai.

Anaya grabs the marker.

**ANAYA:**  
Tum sab side ho jao.

She starts writing fast.

Vihaan checks his phone.

**VIHAAN:**  
Charts main photocopy wale se kara lunga.

Meera cuts pictures from an old notebook while Ishaan searches through a pile of colored paper.

Raghav looks at the chaos.

**RAGHAV:**  
Ye project hai ya kabaddi?

---

## Scene 3 — 0:20–0:30 — Full Jugaad

The group moves around the classroom like a tiny production team.

Aarav holds the chart while Anaya writes. Ishaan sticks pictures crookedly. Kabir keeps smoothing the paper and making it more wrinkled.

**ANAYA:**  
Kabir! Haath hata!

Kabir jumps back.

**KABIR:**  
Main help kar raha hoon.

**ANAYA:**  
Tu bas hawa kar raha hai.

Vihaan suddenly runs in with a packet of printed pages.

**VIHAAN:**  
Printer mil gaya!

Raghav grabs the pages.

**RAGHAV:**  
Ye toh padosi class ka project hai!

Everyone freezes.

Kabir slowly takes one page and turns it upside down.

**KABIR:**  
Same topic hai na.

Anaya stares at him.

**ANAYA:**  
Pagal hai kya?

Kabir grins.

**KABIR:**  
Thoda.

---

## Scene 4 — 0:30–0:40 — Somehow Submitted

Presentation time.

The group walks to the front carrying a crooked chart, loose pages, glue still wet, and one picture hanging by a tiny piece of tape.

Sharma Sir looks at the project.

Then at the group.

**SHARMA SIR:**  
Ye kab banaya?

Everyone looks at each other.

Raghav steps forward.

**RAGHAV:**  
Sir... team effort.

Kabir nods proudly.

**KABIR:**  
Very strong team effort.

The loose picture suddenly falls off the chart.

Everyone watches it drop.

Kabir catches it mid-air and sticks it back on without stopping.

Sharma Sir almost smiles.

**SHARMA SIR:**  
Chalo. Present karo.

Kabir looks at the group.

**KABIR:**  
Kaun karega?

Everyone points at Raghav at the same time.

Raghav closes his eyes.

**RAGHAV:**  
Mujhe pata tha.

**CUT TO BLACK.**

### End Card

> **School project mein kaam ek karta tha... naam sabka hota tha.**
