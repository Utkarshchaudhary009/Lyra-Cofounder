# Rakhi — School Mein Beizzati, Ghar Mein Pyaar

**Type:** Reel  
**Format:** YouTube Short / Animated Reel  
**Estimated Duration:** 40 seconds  
**Series:** Toonseries  
**Language:** UP/Bihar Hinglish  
**Structure:** 4 scenes × 10 seconds

## Premise

Rakhi day starts with school banter, a crush panic, and Kabir being the most harami dost alive. Then the story shifts home, where the same boy who acts irritated around his sister quietly reveals how much that bond actually means.

---

## Scene 1 — 0:00–0:10 — Rakhi Day At School

**Location:** Classroom, morning. Festive energy everywhere. Small rakhis, sweets, colorful threads, bags and water bottles scattered across desks.

Anaya walks in carrying a small box of rakhis. Several classmates gather around her.

Aarav notices her from the back and immediately fixes his hair, then pretends to look at his notebook.

Kabir notices the whole thing.

**KABIR:**  
Aaj toh bhai banega, Aarav.

Aarav looks at him.

**AARAV:**  
Chup kar. Rakhi hai, shaadi nahi.

Kabir laughs loudly.

**KABIR:**  
Arre wah! Banda pehle se defensive hai.

Anaya walks closer with a rakhi in her hand.

Aarav straightens in his seat.

---

## Scene 2 — 0:10–0:20 — Crush Panic + Harami Dost

Anaya stops beside Aarav's desk.

**ANAYA:**  
Aarav, haath de.

Aarav looks at Kabir first.

Kabir silently mouths: "Bhai gaya."

Aarav gives him a warning look, then slowly puts out his hand.

Anaya ties the rakhi neatly.

**ANAYA:**  
Happy Rakhi.

Aarav smiles awkwardly.

**AARAV:**  
Happy... Rakhi.

Kabir immediately leans across the bench.

**KABIR:**  
Kaisa laga, bhaiya?

Aarav kicks his chair.

Kabir nearly falls but catches himself, laughing.

Anaya shakes her head and walks away.

Kabir whispers:

**KABIR:**  
Abey itna serious mat ho. Bhai ban gaya hai tu.

Aarav gives him a dead stare.

---

## Scene 3 — 0:20–0:30 — At Home

Evening. Aarav enters home, drops his school bag near the sofa and removes his shoes.

His younger sister is waiting with a Rakhi plate, trying to look serious.

**SISTER:**  
Idhar aa. Haath de.

Aarav tries to walk past her.

**AARAV:**  
Pehle paani de. School se jaan nikal gayi.

She blocks his way and grabs his wrist.

**SISTER:**  
Pehle Rakhi. Bahut natak karta hai.

Aarav laughs and sits down.

She ties the Rakhi, adjusts it twice because the knot is loose, then puts a small sweet in his mouth.

**AARAV:**  
Bas ek hi?

**SISTER:**  
Pehle gift.

Aarav groans and reaches for his pocket.

---

## Scene 4 — 0:30–0:40 — The Real Bond

Later that night, the house is quiet.

Aarav walks past his sister's room and sees her struggling with a school project at her desk.

He stops, walks in, pulls a chair beside her and takes the paper.

**AARAV:**  
Dikha. Kaise karna hai?

She smiles and moves over.

Aarav starts helping her cut and fold the project while she watches.

**SISTER:**  
Waise gift kya diya?

Aarav silently puts a small wrapped box on her desk.

She opens it and smiles.

**SISTER:**  
Tu chupke se laaya tha?

Aarav shrugs.

**AARAV:**  
Haan toh? Har cheez batani thodi hai.

She leans against his shoulder for a moment.

Aarav looks at the Rakhi on his wrist.

Outside, the house is quiet; a ceiling fan turns slowly and distant neighborhood sounds fill the room.

**CUT TO BLACK.**

### End Card

> **School mein bhai banne ki beizzati alag thi... ghar mein wahi Rakhi sabse pyari lagti thi.**
