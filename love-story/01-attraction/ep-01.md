# Season 1, Episode 1 — The Ordinary Becomes Interesting

**Draft status:** blueprint
**Prose target:** 4,500–5,500 words

## Episode purpose
Introduce Arjun and Mira as complete people before making them a couple-shaped object. Their attraction should be experiential rather than declarative. The audience should leave thinking, "I want to spend more time with these two," which is more important than knowing whether they will date.

## Surface story
A university inter-college festival is in trouble. Arjun is part of the technical/student operations team; Mira is coordinating a literature/debate segment. A cascading set of small problems forces them to cooperate: missing equipment, a schedule conflict, a speaker cancellation, and a power issue. Their first meeting is inconvenient, funny, and practical.

## Scene spine

### Scene 1 — 4:17 p.m.
Arjun is repairing a failing auditorium setup while Kabir gives useless advice. Mira arrives carrying an absurdly overcomplicated schedule. They clash over whose problem is bigger. She needs a technical fix; he needs a schedule fix. Neither initially finds the other impressive.

**Character reveal:** Arjun solves concrete problems quickly but dislikes appearing incompetent. Mira notices details and dislikes being talked over.

### Scene 2 — The Missing Hour
A rehearsal has been accidentally scheduled over another event. Arjun and Mira must negotiate with two student groups who both believe they have priority. They discover they solve conflict differently: Arjun wants a fast compromise; Mira wants to understand why each side cares. The compromise works because they combine methods.

**Subtext:** Their attraction begins as respect, not romance.

### Scene 3 — Tea Stall
After the immediate crisis, they sit with separate friends but end up sharing a table because there are no empty seats. Conversation shifts from event logistics to personal ambitions. Mira challenges Arjun's vague answer about his future. Arjun notices that she is interested in ideas rather than credentials.

**Key line to earn organically:** "You do that a lot—you take a sentence apart until it tells you what the person actually means."

### Scene 4 — The Notebook
Dr. Devika Rao briefly leaves her blue notebook in the committee room. Mira reads a line about relationships being what people do with feelings. Arjun dismisses the idea as too mechanical. Mira counters that feelings cannot make promises on their own. Neither wins.

This is the first philosophical seed. It must feel like a random argument between two students, not a thesis presentation.

### Scene 5 — Rain
The evening ends in sudden rain. Mira has one umbrella; Arjun has a broken one. They share the usable umbrella while walking toward the gate, mostly discussing stupid things. The intimacy is quiet. Neither labels it.

### Scene 6 — The Choice to Continue
The original event reason for their interaction is over. Mira has to leave, then turns back and asks Arjun whether he is free tomorrow to help her reorganize the schedule. He says yes too quickly. She pretends not to notice.

**Episode ending state:** They have created a reason to meet again that is technically practical and emotionally unnecessary.

## Psychological movement
Arjun experiences attraction as a pleasant increase in aliveness. He does not yet think "I need her." He thinks "I like who I am around her." This distinction is important because later he will confuse the two.

Mira experiences the same warmth but is more cautious about interpretation. She recognizes chemistry without believing chemistry tells her what to do.

## Reader learning
The audience should discover that attraction can begin as a change in attention: someone becomes unusually salient, ordinary moments feel sharper, and conversation becomes easier to remember.

## Plant for later
- Arjun jokes about being forgettable.
- Mira jokes that labels are doors that close behind you.
- Kabir observes that Arjun never volunteers for committee work twice unless he likes the people involved.
- Ishaan is mentioned only indirectly as someone Mira already knows from another campus circle.
- The blue notebook is established as an object that will return.

## Ending image
After Mira leaves, Arjun remains under the broken umbrella, watching her disappear into the rain. He is not thinking, "I am in love." He is thinking, "Tomorrow will be better than today." That is enough.
