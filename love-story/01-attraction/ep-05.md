# Season 1, Episode 5 — Other People Exist

**Draft status:** blueprint
**Prose target:** 4,500–5,500 words

## Episode purpose
Break the two-person bubble without breaking the attraction. The audience must experience the subtle shock of realizing that the person you like is not "your character" in a private story. She has old friends, ambitions, family, obligations, and versions of herself that have nothing to do with you.

## Surface story
A weekend inter-college event brings multiple social groups together. Mira's old friend Ishaan becomes more prominent. Arjun meets Mira's friends and she meets Arjun's. Their worlds begin overlapping.

## Scene spine

### Scene 1 — The group plan
Mira invites Arjun to a group outing. He expects a quieter day together and is mildly disappointed when the group grows larger. His disappointment is not yet jealousy; it is the first sign that he has begun assigning value to exclusive access.

### Scene 2 — Ishaan's ease
Ishaan knows old stories about Mira. Their jokes are efficient because of years of shared context. Arjun initially enjoys the stories, then realizes there are entire chapters of her life in which he did not exist.

### Scene 3 — Mira's old mistake
A story about Mira from school contradicts Arjun's image of her. She once abandoned a safe opportunity to pursue something uncertain. Arjun admires this, but also sees that her independent decisions may repeatedly compete with closeness.

### Scene 4 — Kabir and Arjun
Kabir teases Arjun about behaving as though Mira is "his person." Arjun rejects the phrase because they are not dating. Kabir asks whether labels create ownership or merely reveal expectations that already exist.

### Scene 5 — Mira meets Rhea
Rhea, a direct and practical student involved in Arjun's product work, meets Mira. She likes Mira immediately but notices the ambiguity between them. Rhea refuses to call them a couple because nobody has chosen that.

### Scene 6 — The first small exclusion
Mira chooses to spend an hour with Ishaan because he needs help with something personal. She tells Arjun she will meet him later. He says it is fine. It is not fine internally. He hides the disappointment instead of discussing it.

### Scene 7 — Naina sees it
Naina observes that Mira senses Arjun's disappointment but does not know what responsibility she has for it. She asks Mira whether she wants closeness with Arjun or the comfort of being important to him. Mira refuses to answer.

### Scene 8 — The return
Arjun and Mira meet later that evening. They still enjoy one another. Their affection has not diminished. The difference is that the audience now sees that affection operates inside a larger network of relationships and priorities.

**Episode ending state:** Arjun recognizes that being important to Mira does not mean being central to her entire life. The realization attracts him and unsettles him simultaneously.

## Psychological movement
Arjun's attraction begins shifting toward exclusivity without his permission. Mira begins noticing that emotional closeness creates expectations even when nobody has named them.

## Reader learning
A person does not become available merely because another person feels strongly about them. Attraction happens between whole lives, not blank spaces.

## Plant for later
- Ishaan's history becomes a major ambiguity source without turning him into an antagonist.
- Rhea's practical worldview is seeded.
- Arjun learns that disappointment can be hidden and still affect a relationship.
- Mira begins learning that honesty about confusion does not automatically make confusion harmless.
