# Season 1, Episode 6 — Say It Without Naming It

**Draft status:** blueprint
**Prose target:** 4,500–5,500 words

## Episode purpose
Make the relationship acquire obligations before it acquires a name. The characters should discover that they already have expectations of one another, and that expectations create the possibility of hurt even without formal commitment.

## Surface story
Arjun and Mira are each busy with separate commitments. A string of missed messages and changed plans creates the first serious conflict. Nobody lies. Nobody betrays anyone. The conflict comes from different assumptions about what closeness entitles each person to expect.

## Scene spine

### Scene 1 — A changed plan
Mira cancels a plan at short notice because of a family matter. Arjun says it is fine but rearranges his own schedule around it. He does not tell her that he did so.

### Scene 2 — The delayed message
Hours later, Mira messages casually. Arjun's response is colder than usual. She immediately senses it but cannot identify what she did wrong.

### Scene 3 — Kabir's advice
Kabir argues that Arjun should "stop expecting relationship behavior from a non-relationship." Arjun objects that they do many relationship-like things already. Kabir's point is not that Arjun is wrong, but that the ambiguity is allowing each person to write private rules.

### Scene 4 — Mira and Naina
Mira explains the situation. Naina points out that Mira also has an expectation: she wants Arjun available emotionally without wanting to promise that she will always be available in return.

Mira becomes defensive, then quiet.

### Scene 5 — The confrontation
Arjun and Mira finally speak directly. Arjun says, "I don't need a label. I need to know whether what matters to me matters to you." Mira responds, "It matters. I just don't know what I'm allowed to promise."

This should be the emotional center of the episode.

### Scene 6 — The dangerous repair
They agree to be more honest whenever plans change and whenever one of them feels hurt. This sounds mature and works temporarily. The audience should feel relief.

But they do not solve the deeper problem: neither knows what level of commitment the other is moving toward.

### Scene 7 — A quiet walk
They walk home and return to their normal rhythm. Their ability to repair conflict makes them closer. That closeness increases emotional dependency and therefore raises future stakes.

**Episode ending state:** They now have a private rule about communication. They are closer, but the hidden expectations have become more explicit.

## Psychological movement
Arjun learns that unspoken expectations can create resentment. Mira learns that her uncertainty has consequences even when she is sincere about it.

Neither fully accepts those conclusions yet.

## Reader learning
A relationship is not defined only by labels. It is also defined by repeated expectations, permissions, boundaries, and the consequences attached to violating them.

## Plant for later
- The communication pact should later be broken during a major crisis.
- "I don't know what I'm allowed to promise" becomes a central line for Mira's character arc.
- Arjun's habit of sacrificing silently should become a recurring source of resentment.
- Kabir's low-expectation philosophy gains credibility because it seems to prevent this exact problem.

## Ending image
Arjun and Mira part at the gate. She turns back and says, "Same tea tomorrow?" He smiles. The ritual survives. Neither notices that their private world is becoming something that can now be damaged.
