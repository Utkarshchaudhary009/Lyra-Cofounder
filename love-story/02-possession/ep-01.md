# Episode 1 — The Small Change

> **Status:** Outline / scene blueprint. Full prose must be 4,000+ words.

## Episode purpose
Move Arjun and Mira from the easy warmth of Season 1 into the first subtle experience of loss. Nothing objectively bad happens. The change is tiny. The important event is Arjun discovering that his internal state can change even when Mira has done nothing wrong.

## Opening
A familiar routine between Arjun and Mira is interrupted by something mundane: Mira becomes absorbed in a new project and starts spending more time with a different group. Arjun initially jokes about it. He expects the routine to resume.

## Scene 1 — The missing chair
At their usual place, Mira is absent because she is helping another team. Arjun tells himself it is insignificant. His attention keeps returning to the empty place beside him.

**Character movement:** Arjun learns that absence can occupy attention more than presence.

## Scene 2 — The new rhythm
Mira returns later, energized by her work. She talks freely about a new person in the group, Ishaan, without realizing the name will matter. Arjun is genuinely interested, then asks one question too many.

Mira teases him about interviewing her. He laughs and changes subject.

**Subtext:** his curiosity is partly a request for reassurance.

## Scene 3 — Evidence gathering
Arjun notices small details: how quickly Mira replies to Ishaan's message, an inside joke, how naturally she asks him for help. None is romantic. Arjun nevertheless stores them.

Naina catches him watching and asks what he is doing. He says, "Nothing."

She does not challenge him.

## Scene 4 — The return to normal
Mira seeks Arjun out later. Their old rhythm returns immediately. He feels relief and interprets it as proof that nothing was wrong.

But the relief itself reveals that something had been wrong inside him.

## Ending image
Arjun receives a message from Mira: "Busy tomorrow. Don't wait for me."

He types "Okay." Deletes it. Types another version. Eventually sends the first.

He puts the phone down, then picks it up again.

**End state:** no relationship has changed externally. Arjun's relationship with uncertainty has changed.

## Future seeds
- Ishaan's familiarity with Mira.
- Arjun's habit of storing tiny observations.
- Naina's ability to see without intervening.
- The phrase "Don't wait for me" returns later with a different meaning.
