# Love Story — Master Architecture

A long-form philosophical romance designed as a story first and a philosophy laboratory underneath.

## Format

- 10 seasons
- 6–7 episodes per season
- Every episode: **4,000+ words minimum**
- Approximate total: 240,000–280,000+ words

## Core principle

The story should never stop to explain its philosophy. Characters act from incomplete models of love; consequences expose contradictions; the reader gradually updates their model of reality.

## Seasons

1. Attraction — What does attraction feel like, and what do we mistake it for?
2. Possession — When does care become ownership or control?
3. Rejection — What does another person's no actually mean?
4. Substitution — Can changing the person change the pattern?
5. Collision — What happens when incompatible models of love meet?
6. Consequence — What do beliefs cost when they become behavior?
7. Separation — Who are we when the relationship is removed?
8. Recognition — Can we see the patterns we keep repeating?
9. Responsibility — What does loving someone obligate us to do, and what does it not?
10. Choice — What do we choose when every honest choice has a cost?

## Writing rule

> Show the philosophy. Never announce the lesson before the characters have earned it.
