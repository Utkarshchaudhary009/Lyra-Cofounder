# Master Character Arc

## Arjun

**Beginning:** Believes strong feeling is evidence. He wants to be chosen and interprets ambiguity as a problem to solve.

**Pressure:** Jealousy, rejection, substitution, and relationship failure repeatedly expose the cost of using another person to stabilize self-worth.

**Middle:** Learns to distinguish emotion from entitlement and intention from consequence. Insight arrives unevenly; relapse is expected.

**End:** Can hold love and uncertainty together without converting uncertainty into control. Makes choices based on reality and accepts their costs.

## Mira

**Beginning:** Treats emotional honesty as sufficient fairness. She values freedom but underestimates the obligations created by prolonged ambiguity.

**Pressure:** Her desire to preserve closeness without definition repeatedly harms people she genuinely cares about.

**Middle:** Learns that avoiding a choice is still a choice, and that freedom includes allowing others to make informed choices based on the truth.

**End:** Can commit deliberately, decline honestly, or leave responsibly without needing to make any of those choices morally superior.

## Kabir

**Beginning:** Uses humor and low expectations to keep relationships reversible.

**Pressure:** A serious relationship makes his avoidance costly.

**End:** Learns that emotional independence is not the same as emotional non-responsibility.

## Naina

**Beginning:** Understands everyone else's motives and quietly compensates for them.

**Pressure:** Repeatedly becomes the person who absorbs costs created by other people's uncertainty.

**End:** Learns to care without managing other people.

## Ishaan

**Beginning:** Believes undefined intimacy can preserve connection without harm.

**Pressure:** Sees how ambiguity can keep multiple people from moving freely.

**End:** Learns that clarity can be kinder than perpetual possibility.

## Rhea

**Beginning:** Believes compatibility and life plans can rationally solve most relationship problems.

**Pressure:** Encounters genuine emotion that does not fit a clean optimization model.

**End:** Learns to reason with both values and emotion without letting either become absolute.

## Dr. Rao

**Beginning:** Appears to be the philosophical authority.

**Revelation:** Her own history contains unresolved contradictions, preventing her from becoming an omniscient teacher.

**End:** She remains a question-asker rather than a person with the final answer.

## Ensemble progression

The cast should not move in synchronized lockstep. One character may understand possession while another is still romanticizing it. This creates dramatic irony and prevents the narrative from feeling like a syllabus.
