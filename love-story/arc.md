# Season Arc

## Episode 1 — Attraction
Arjun and Mira meet while organizing a university literary-tech festival. Their first connection is easy and pleasurable. Kabir jokes that the equation is obvious. Mira's former almost-relationship with Ishaan is still unresolved in the background. The episode ends when Arjun writes in the blue notebook: "Love is when one person's presence improves the quality of an ordinary day."

## Episode 2 — Possession
Mira becomes distant during a chaotic week. Arjun notices her laughing with Ishaan and experiences jealousy he interprets as proof of depth. Mira is unsettled when another student grows close to Arjun. They both use jealousy as evidence that the bond is real. Their first serious conflict reveals that wanting access to another person is not the same as being entitled to it.

## Episode 3 — Rejection
Mira refuses Arjun's attempt to define the relationship. Arjun says he respects the answer, then behaves in ways designed to demonstrate that he is moving on. Mira discovers she is jealous. Everyone around them interprets this differently. Arjun faces the first hard distinction between being rejected and being wronged.

## Episode 4 — Substitution
Kabir becomes involved with a new person, Rhea, after an earlier rejection. He insists that his feelings prove each relationship is unique, but the same triggers, jokes, anxieties, and tests reappear. Arjun also begins talking to someone else. The audience sees the structural loop before the characters do.

## Episode 5 — Collision
The university festival brings everyone into one social orbit. Small private acts create public consequences. A screenshot, a missed call, a half-told story, and a protective lie combine. Mira accuses Arjun of not trusting her; Arjun accuses Mira of wanting to keep him available without choosing him. Both are partly right. The episode ends with a friendship breaking more than the romance does.

## Episode 6 — Consequence
Naina's sacrificial model takes center stage. She tries to hold a fractured friend group together by making choices for people "for their own good." Arjun sees himself in her. Mira sees herself in Arjun. An attempt at reconciliation becomes coercive without meaning to. The characters learn that good intentions do not erase another person's agency.

## Episode 7 — Separation
Time passes. Contact drops. Arjun has no relationship to perform and no one to persuade. Mira's life continues without him. Kabir's new relationship is quieter and therefore harder for him to understand. The characters discover who they are when the emotional object around which they organized themselves is absent.

## Episode 8 — Recognition
A chance reunion makes the old pattern visible. Same arguments, different wording. Same need, different person. Kabir finally admits that he does not move on; he relocates. Arjun recognizes that much of his love was an attempt to prevent feeling replaceable. Mira realizes that intensity made some relationships feel truer than they were.

## Episode 9 — Responsibility
Arjun and Mira consider whether a renewed relationship is even compatible with the lives they are becoming. They confront an uncomfortable possibility: love may survive even when partnership should not. Choice is tested against career, family expectations, geography, values, and personal boundaries. No character gets the painless option.

## Episode 10 — Choice
The final episode refuses a fantasy reunion. Instead, the characters make explicit choices and accept their consequences. Arjun and Mira have one final honest conversation. "I choose you" is reinterpreted: not as possession or destiny, but as a present act that includes the freedom to say no. The final image echoes Episode 1 but changes its meaning.
