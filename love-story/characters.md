# Characters

## Arjun Mehra — 20
Engineering student, sharp, socially observant, impatient with abstractions. He believes he is unusually rational but repeatedly uses rational language to defend emotional decisions. His working theory is simple: love begins when being with someone feels unusually good, and a good relationship is one that keeps both people happy. His flaw is not selfishness alone; it is his habit of treating another person's reaction as evidence about his own worth.

**Wants:** to feel chosen without having to ask to be chosen.
**Fear:** being replaceable.
**Contradiction:** claims to respect freedom but becomes competitive whenever attention shifts away from him.
**Arc:** from identifying love with emotional reward to understanding choice, reciprocity, and responsibility.

## Mira Sanyal — 20
Literature student, perceptive, articulate, emotionally intense but not always emotionally clear. She sincerely believes feelings are the most honest evidence available. She distrusts people who call love a "decision" because she thinks that language can hide lifeless relationships. Yet she sometimes mistakes intensity and jealousy for depth.

**Wants:** a relationship that feels undeniable.
**Fear:** becoming ordinary to someone she once mattered to.
**Contradiction:** demands honesty while sometimes using ambiguity to keep options open.
**Arc:** from trusting emotional intensity as truth to distinguishing feeling from obligation and care.

## Kabir Saxena — 21
Arjun's closest friend. Funny, impulsive, serially optimistic. After rejection he quickly redirects himself toward another person and calls it "moving on." He is the clearest demonstration of the season's loop: different faces, same need for validation.

**Wants:** novelty and proof that he is desirable.
**Fear:** sitting alone with an ordinary evening.
**Contradiction:** thinks he is emotionally detached but needs other people to confirm the story he tells about himself.
**Arc:** from substitution to recognition.

## Naina Kapoor — 21
Mira's friend. Practical and apparently mature. She believes love is sacrifice: if you truly love someone, you make room, compromise, and endure inconvenience. She is often right, but her theory becomes dangerous when she uses suffering as evidence that a relationship matters.

**Wants:** to be indispensable.
**Fear:** being cared for without being needed.
**Contradiction:** calls control "care" whenever sacrifice gives her moral leverage.

## Sameer Ali — 22
A senior student who has already had one long relationship end. He is the season's quiet counterpoint. He believes freedom and responsibility must coexist. He has no perfect formula and resists being made the wise character. His own failure is that he sometimes becomes so afraid of controlling others that he avoids asking for what he needs.

**Wants:** a life that remains his own inside partnership.
**Fear:** becoming either a cage or a ghost.
**Contradiction:** mistakes emotional distance for respect.

## Ishaan Verma — 20
Mira's former almost-relationship and later friend. He is important because he refuses the role of villain. His choices are sometimes hurtful, but his motives are legible. He becomes a living challenge to Arjun's instinct to reduce complicated behavior to a single hidden motive.

**Wants:** clarity.
**Fear:** being trapped inside other people's interpretations of him.

## The Professor
Dr. Devika Rao, who teaches a seminar on stories and ethics. She rarely explains the characters' lives, but occasionally provides a question that opens a door rather than closing it. Her function is to model inquiry, not wisdom-as-authority.
