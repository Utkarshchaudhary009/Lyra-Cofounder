## Core relationships

### Arjun ↔ Mira
Starts as mutual enjoyment and curiosity. Neither initially understands the difference between strong feeling and a relationship. Their ambiguity creates the central pressure system.

### Mira ↔ Ishaan
A meaningful past connection that resists clean categorization. It functions as an alternative model: intimacy can exist without formal commitment—but ambiguity also has costs.

### Arjun ↔ Rhea
Begins as functional collaboration and later tests the substitution hypothesis. Rhea is not a replacement character; she exposes which parts of Arjun's attraction are person-specific and which are recurring needs.

### Arjun ↔ Kabir
Friendship used to reveal rationalization. Kabir can say what Arjun refuses to admit, but Kabir's own avoidance prevents him from being a simple wise friend.

### Mira ↔ Naina
A friendship that explores the difference between understanding someone and enabling them. Naina gradually stops translating Mira's uncertainty into excuses.

### Naina ↔ Everyone
Naina is often close enough to see patterns before the people involved do, but she must learn not to turn perception into control.

## Relationship evolution rule

Every important relationship should change at least once because of a choice, not merely because time passed. The change must produce new constraints for later seasons.
