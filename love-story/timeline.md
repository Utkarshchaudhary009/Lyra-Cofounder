# Timeline

## Phase I — University: discovery

**Months 1–6:** Season 1–2 territory. Arjun and Mira meet through campus work. Friendship becomes attraction. Social networks form. Ambiguity and jealousy emerge.

**Months 7–12:** Season 3–4 territory. Rejection and substitution enter the system. Characters experiment with distance, new people, and new interpretations.

## Phase II — University to early adulthood: collision

**Year 2:** Season 5–6 territory. Career choices, internships, family expectations, and geographic possibilities put pressure on relationships. Consequences accumulate.

**Year 3:** Season 7 territory. Major separation. The characters' lives begin diverging materially, not just emotionally.

## Phase III — Early professional life: recognition

**Years 4–6:** Season 8–9 territory. Characters encounter adult versions of old patterns. New relationships and responsibilities force them to apply what they learned without assuming the past will repeat exactly.

## Phase IV — Choice

**Years 6–8:** Season 10. The characters meet again under materially different conditions. The final conflict is not nostalgia; it is whether their current lives can honestly coexist.

## Continuity rules

- Time must have consequences. Skills, careers, friendships, habits, and worldviews evolve.
- Characters should not retain identical emotional language across eight years.
- Old scenes can acquire new meaning when revisited.
- Major life transitions happen between episodes as well as within them.
- No time skip may exist solely to move the plot to a convenient age; it must create changed conditions.
